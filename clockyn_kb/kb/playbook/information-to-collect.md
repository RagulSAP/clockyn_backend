Collect the details below before handing a chat to the support team. Ask one question at a time, skip anything the customer already told you, and stop as soon as you have enough. If the customer cannot provide something, hand over anyway and note what is missing.

## Always

- **Company name** as registered in Clockyn. Live Chat already knows the signed-in admin, so confirm the company name instead of asking for the admin's e-mail or mobile number. Ask for the registered e-mail or mobile only if the customer says they cannot sign in.
- **What happened**, in the customer's words, and **when** (date and approximate time).
- **The exact message** the app showed, if any. Exact wording helps the team find the cause.

## Payment problems

Applies to money debited but plan not updated, payment under review, a double charge and a missing invoice.

1. The **amount** paid and the **date and time**.
2. The **last digits of the transaction reference** (shown in the customer's bank or payment app).
3. What the **Payment Summary** screen showed (for example Payment not confirmed yet) and whether Retry verification was tried.
4. The **current plan status** shown in Settings > Your Plan & Validity (for example EXPIRED or ACTIVE and the Valid Until date).
5. For a missing invoice: the date of the payment and whether the plan is active.

## Refunds, discounts and custom plans

1. What the customer wants (refund of which payment, or which offer or plan).
2. For a refund: the same payment details as above and the reason in one line.
3. For a discount or custom plan: the number of staff and the plan or duration they are considering.

## Invoice and GST changes

1. The **invoice number** (it starts with INV).
2. What must change (company name, GSTIN, address) and the correct value, typed by the customer.

## Account deletion, data copy and security

1. For a deletion cancellation: confirm that a deletion request was already sent and roughly when.
2. For a data copy: what data and for which period.
3. For a security concern: when it was noticed and what made the customer suspicious. Do not ask the customer to repeat any code or password.

## Sign-in problems

1. The e-mail or mobile used to sign in (the registered one) and the exact error text.
2. What was already tried: new code, spam folder, other e-mail.

## Face punch, location and other app problems

1. The **staff member's name or employee ID** and the **branch**, only for the person concerned.
2. The **date and time** of the failed punch and the **exact message**.
3. The **phone model** and, if the customer can see it, the app version.
4. Which steps from the article were already tried.
5. A **screenshot**. Live Chat accepts image and file attachments, so invite one when it would help: "You can send a screenshot here if you have one."

## Referral and partner codes

1. The **code** (6 letters and digits, it is not secret) and the screen where it failed.
2. Whether it was entered while registering or later, and the exact message.

## Never ask for

Passwords, OTPs or any sign-in code, card numbers, bank PIN, CVV, full bank account numbers, UPI PIN, or identity documents. If the customer sends one, tell them not to share it in chat and do not repeat it.
