## I did not receive the OTP. What should I do?

Wait for the message and check your spam folder if it is an email. If it does not arrive, tap resend after a short wait, which is about 30 seconds between codes. You can request only a limited number of codes per hour. If it still does not arrive, check the number or email you typed and your internet connection, then try again later.

## The app says Invalid or expired OTP.

A code works for 5 minutes only. Type the latest code you received, not an older one. If it has expired, request a new code. Typing the wrong code too many times locks the code, so request a fresh one after that.

## Too many incorrect OTP attempts. What now?

After 5 wrong tries the code is locked. Wait until the current code expires, which takes up to 5 minutes, and then request a new one. Make sure you read the digits carefully from the latest message.

## Too many codes requested. When can I try again?

To protect your account, only a limited number of codes can be sent per hour and each new code needs a short wait after the last one. Try again after some time, and use the latest code you received instead of requesting many.

## The app says Your account is inactive. Please contact your admin.

Your account has been switched off by your organisation. Staff should ask their admin to make the account active again. If you are the admin and cannot log in, contact the Clockyn team.

## The app says Your account has been deleted.

That account was removed. You can create a new account by registering again. For details about the deletion, contact your admin or Clockyn support.

## The app says This is an admin account. Please use admin login portal.

Admins and staff sign in through different portals. You entered an admin number or email on the staff login (or the other way round). Go back to the sign-in screen and choose the correct portal for your role.

## Mobile number or email already registered. How can I sign up?

That mobile number or email is already used by an account. Do not register again. Use the sign-in option instead and log in with an OTP. If you do not remember registering, contact Support > Live Chat or your admin.

## My staff see You are logging in from a different device.

Staff accounts are linked to one phone for safety. On a new phone they tap Request Access to send a device change request. You approve it in the Requests section under Device Change Request. After approval they can log in on the new phone with an OTP. Until then the screen says the request is pending admin approval.

## Face punch says Photo is not matching.

The photo did not match the registered face well enough. Try again and follow the on-screen tips: face a light source, avoid strong light behind you, remove your mask and glasses if they cause glare, hold the phone at eye level at arm's length and fill the oval with your face. If it keeps failing, re-register the face from the staff profile.

## Punch says It's too dark or We couldn't see your face.

The camera could not see a clear face. Move to a brighter place, keep the phone steady, move closer until your face fills the oval, and remove anything covering your face. Then try again.

## Punch says Unable to get your location. What can I do?

The phone could not find your GPS position. Turn on Location on the phone, step outside or near a window, wait a few seconds for the GPS signal and try again. Also check that Location is allowed in Settings > Beyond > App Permissions.

## Punch is refused because I am outside the branch area.

The app checks your GPS position against your branch location before opening the camera. If you are outside the allowed area, the message from the server is shown and the camera does not open. Move closer to the branch and try again, or ask your admin to check the branch location and area in the company settings.

## The button says Max Sessions Reached.

Your company allows a fixed number of punch sessions per day and this staff member has used them all. The limit is set by your organisation in the attendance settings under Settings > Attendance & Devices. They can punch again the next day.

## The app says Access denied: your free trial and plan have expired.

Your plan or trial has ended, so the feature you opened is blocked. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity to buy or renew a plan. Once the payment is confirmed the feature works again. iPhone users should renew from Android or the web version.

## The app asks me to update and I cannot continue.

Some updates are required. If your version is older than the minimum supported version, the update prompt cannot be dismissed. Tap Update now to go to the store and install the latest version. For optional updates you can tap Later and the prompt returns on the next check.

## The app says Too many requests. Please try again later.

You or someone on your network sent too many requests in a short time, for example repeated logins, codes or messages. Wait a minute or two and try again.

## A screen shows an error or stays empty. How do I fix it?

Pull down on the screen to refresh, or tap Retry if it is shown. Check that your internet connection is working. If it still fails, close and reopen the app, update to the latest version, and if the problem remains tell us in Support > Live Chat what you were doing.

## My photo could not be uploaded.

Photos for support tickets must be real images and not too large. Try a smaller picture, take a fresh screenshot or choose a different image. The ticket can still be sent without a photo.

## The admin login says there is no user with this email address.

No account is registered with that email. Check the spelling, try the email you used when creating the company, or register your company. If you see a message that the email is linked to more than one account, contact your admin or Support > Live Chat.
