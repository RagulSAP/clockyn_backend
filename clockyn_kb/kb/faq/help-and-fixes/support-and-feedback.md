## How can I contact Clockyn support?

Open the Support tab. You will find FAQ for answers, Live Chat to talk to our support team, and Video Hub for step-by-step guides. To report a problem or ask for help, use Live Chat. Support options are available to organisation admins.

## What is Live Chat and who can use it?

Live Chat lets you talk directly to the Clockyn support team from the Support tab. It is available to organisation admins. Messages from our team reach you as notifications and the Live Chat row shows an unread badge until you open it. Our team may also message you first, for example about your plan or a question you raised: the message appears in Support > Live Chat and you can reply there. It is the best place to ask about billing, refunds or special requests.

## What is the Video Hub?

Video Hub in the Support tab has step-by-step guide videos on how to use the app. Tap a video to play it full screen. New videos appear there as soon as the Clockyn team adds them.

## Why can I not see the Support tab?

The Support tab with FAQ, Live Chat and Video Hub is meant for organisation admins. Staff and managers do not see it. Sign in with the admin account to use it.

## How do I suggest a new feature?

1. Open Settings > Beyond > Suggest a Feature.
2. Type a short title, for example Add dark mode support.
3. Describe your idea in detail.
4. Tap Send Suggestion.
You will see Thank you, your suggestion has been sent. You can send up to 10 suggestions per hour.

## How do I request deletion of my account?

1. Open Settings > Beyond > More Options.
2. Open Privacy Policy.
3. Tap Request Account Deletion and confirm with Submit Request.
The request is sent for review, and once it is reviewed, all the data is permanently deleted. After you send it, the app signs you out.

## What should I tell support so they can help me faster?

Mention what you were trying to do, what the screen said (the exact message helps), your company name and the time it happened. For payment issues add the amount, the date and time and the last digits of the transaction reference. A screenshot sent in Live Chat also helps.

## Where can I see the Fine / Deduction feature?

Fine / Deduction appears under Settings > Salary Structure, but it is not ready yet and shows Coming soon when you tap it. Use Suggest a Feature if you want to tell us how you would like it to work.
