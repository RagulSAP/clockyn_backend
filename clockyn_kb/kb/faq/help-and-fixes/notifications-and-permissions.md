## Which permissions does the app need and why?

Location: GPS check-in and attendance tracking. Camera: face recognition and photo capture. Notifications: alerts, reminders and updates. Photos: attach images to requests and expenses. You can see and change all four under Settings > Beyond > App Permissions.

## Where do I check or change app permissions?

1. Open Settings.
2. Tap Beyond, then App Permissions.
You will see a card for Location, Camera, Notifications and Photos, with a summary like 3 of 4 permissions granted and a badge that says All Active or the number Pending. Tap a card to allow it.

## What happens when I tap a permission card?

If the permission was never asked, the phone shows its normal permission prompt. If you already allowed it, nothing needs to be done. If you had blocked it earlier, the app opens your phone settings so you can switch it on, and the status updates when you come back to the app.

## Why does the app ask for location Allow all the time on Android?

On Android the app also asks for the all the time location option so that attendance tracking can work in the background. iPhones do not ask for this extra step. If you refuse it, the app opens the phone settings so you can change it.

## How do I allow camera, location or notifications in a web browser?

A browser does not let the app open its settings for you. The app shows a message telling you to allow the permission for this site in your browser's site settings, usually from the lock icon next to the web address. Allow it and then reload the page.

## Why am I not getting notifications on my phone?

Check these in order:
1. Settings > Beyond > App Permissions: Notifications should be allowed.
2. Make sure the phone itself has notifications on for Clockyn and that Do Not Disturb or battery saver is not blocking them.
3. Check your internet connection.
4. Log out and log in again so the phone registers for alerts again.

## What notifications will I receive as an admin?

You are alerted when staff punch in or out, when a staff member submits a request such as leave or a device change, when someone sends you a chat message or a group message, and when our support team replies in Live Chat. Tapping a notification opens the matching screen.

## What notifications do my staff receive?

Staff are alerted about announcements, new chat and group messages, new polls and decisions on their requests. Tapping the alert opens the related screen in their app.

## What happens when I tap a notification?

The app opens the screen the alert is about. Punch alerts open the home screen, request alerts open the Requests section, chat alerts open the conversation, and Live Chat alerts open Support > Live Chat. If the alert is of a type the app does not recognise, nothing special opens.

## Why did I not get a notification when staff punched in?

Punch alerts go to the phones of your admins. Make sure Notifications are allowed for the app, the phone is online, and you are logged in on that phone. You can always check punches from the home screen or the attendance screens without the alert.

## Do Live Chat replies from the support team send a notification?

Yes. When our team replies in Live Chat, or sends you a message first, admins receive a notification and the Live Chat row in Support shows an unread badge. Live Chat is available only to organisation admins.

## How do I stop notifications or turn them off?

Switch Notifications off for Clockyn in your phone settings. The App Permissions screen shows the current status but a blocked permission can only be changed in the phone's own settings. Note that you may then miss punch alerts, requests and chat messages.

## Why does the app need access to my photos?

Photos permission lets you attach images to requests and expenses. If you deny it, the app shows Please allow photo library access when you try to pick a picture. Allow it from Settings > Beyond > App Permissions.

## Why does the app say Allow location to punch in?

Punching checks that you are inside your branch area, so location permission is needed first. The app says Clockyn needs your location to verify you are within your branch and asks you to enable Location access in Settings. Allow it in App Permissions and try again.

## Why does the camera not open when I punch?

Face punch needs the camera permission and a working location check. The app checks the permission, then GPS, then your branch area, and only then opens the camera. Check Settings > Beyond > App Permissions that Camera and Location are both allowed.
