## How do I pay for my plan?

After you pick a plan and a staff count, the Payment Summary screen opens. Tap Continue to Pay and finish the payment in the payment window that opens. When the payment is confirmed you will see a success message and your plan details update.

## How do I apply a coupon code?

1. On the Payment Summary screen find the coupon box.
2. Type your code in Enter coupon code and apply it.
3. If it is valid, the discount shows in the price breakdown and the Grand Total updates.
Coupons apply to the staff charge after any plan credit. They are not available for the add-staff Upgrade Plan flow.

## Why is my coupon not working?

A coupon can be rejected for these reasons: the code is wrong or expired, it is not active yet, it is not valid for the plan you selected, or your order is below the coupon's minimum amount. The message under the coupon box says which one applies. Check the code spelling, try a different plan, or ask Support > Live Chat for a valid code.

## Is the coupon discount percentage or a fixed amount?

It depends on the coupon. Some give a percentage off and some give a fixed rupee amount, and a percentage coupon may have an upper limit on the discount. The discount is applied before GST. The Payment Summary shows exactly how much you saved.

## I closed the payment window. Was I charged?

If you close the window before paying, the app shows Payment Cancelled and nothing is charged. If you did pay and closed the window afterwards, wait a few minutes and check Settings > Your Plan & Validity. If your bank shows a debit but the plan did not change, see the question about money debited and plan not updated.

## What does Verification Failed or Payment not confirmed yet mean?

It means the payment may have gone through but the app could not confirm it with our server, for example because of a network drop. Do not pay again. On the Payment Summary tap Retry verification. If it still fails, note the payment time and amount and send them to us in Support > Live Chat.

## Money was debited but my plan did not update. What should I do?

First open the Payment Summary again and tap Retry verification if it is shown. Our system also gets a confirmation directly from the payment provider, so a successful payment is normally applied automatically within a short time. If the plan is still not updated after a while, contact us in Support > Live Chat with the date, time, amount and the last digits of the transaction reference.

## Why is my payment shown as under review?

When the amount we receive does not match the amount we expected for the order, the payment is held for a check by our team instead of activating the plan automatically. You do not need to pay again. Share the details in Support > Live Chat and we will sort it out.

## Will I be charged twice if I tap pay again?

Each time you start a payment a new payment order is created, so a second attempt is a second charge if it succeeds. If you see Payment not confirmed yet or Verification Failed, use Retry verification instead of paying again. If you were charged twice by mistake, contact Support > Live Chat.

## Where can I find my invoices?

Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity and tap Download Invoices. The Invoices screen has two tabs: Invoice History, which lists every invoice from your successful payments, and Downloaded, which lists the invoice files saved on this device.

## How do I download an invoice?

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity > Download Invoices.
2. Stay on the Invoice History tab and pick the invoice.
3. Choose to download it. You will see a message that the invoice was downloaded successfully.
The file then appears in the Downloaded tab, where you can open the PDF, or share it.

## How do I share an invoice by email or WhatsApp?

Open Download Invoices, go to the Downloaded tab and pick the invoice. Choose the share option (Send via email, messaging apps & more) and select the app you want, such as your email app or a messaging app. If you do not see the invoice there, download it first from the Invoice History tab.

## The app says No PDF viewer found. What do I do?

Your phone has no app that can open PDF files. Install a PDF reader from your app store, or use the share option on the invoice to send it to your email or another device and open it there.

## How do I delete a downloaded invoice?

In Download Invoices, open the Downloaded tab, pick the invoice and choose Remove from this device. Confirm Delete Invoice. This only removes the file from your phone. The invoice stays in Invoice History, so you can download it again whenever you need.

## What does the invoice number look like and what GST details does it show?

Invoice numbers look like INV-2025-0001, which is the word INV, the year and a running number. The invoice shows your plan, the amount and the GST split, either as CGST and SGST or as IGST. Use these details for your accounts and GST filing.

## I do not see an invoice for my last payment.

Invoices are created only after a payment is successfully confirmed. If your payment is still unconfirmed or under review, the invoice will not appear yet. Pull down to refresh the Invoice History tab. If the plan is active but the invoice is missing, contact Support > Live Chat.

## Can I get a refund?

Refund rules are decided by the Clockyn team and are not set inside the app. Please write to us in Support > Live Chat with your payment details and we will tell you what is possible.
