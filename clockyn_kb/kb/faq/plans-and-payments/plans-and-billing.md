## Where can I see my current plan and its validity?

Open Settings and tap Your Plan & Validity (Manage subscription). The top card shows your plan status badge (FREE TRIAL, ACTIVE, EXPIRED or NO PLAN) with a short sentence about it. Below it you can see Billing Date, Valid Until, Staff Limit, Used Staff and Remaining staff seats, plus a usage ring that shows how full your staff limit is.

## What do Billing Date, Valid Until, Staff Limit, Used Staff and Remaining mean?

Billing Date is the day your current plan started. Valid Until is the last day you can use it; the plan still works on that day itself. Staff Limit is the number of staff your plan allows, Used Staff is how many active staff you already have, and Remaining is the difference, which is how many more staff you can add.

## What do the FREE TRIAL, ACTIVE, EXPIRED and NO PLAN badges mean?

FREE TRIAL means you are still in the starter period that comes with a new company. ACTIVE means you have a paid plan that is still valid. EXPIRED means your paid plan has passed its Valid Until date. NO PLAN means the free trial is over and you have not bought a plan yet. The card under the badge explains what to do next.

## How long is the free trial and what does it include?

Every new company starts with a free trial of 1 month. During the trial all features are available, and you can add up to 10 staff members. When the month is over you need to buy a plan to keep using the features. Your Valid Until date for the trial is shown under Settings > Your Plan & Validity.

## What happens when my free trial or plan expires?

Your data stays safe, but the features that need a plan are switched off until you renew or buy a plan. This includes face punch with the camera, chat, salary structure, staff location tracking and CRM, and the app tells you that your free trial and plan have expired and asks you to subscribe. Buy or renew from Settings > Your Plan & Validity and everything works again.

## How do I buy a plan for the first time?

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity.
2. Tap Plan Details. Under each plan (Core, Prime, Elite) scroll sideways to see its durations, for example 1 Month, 6 Months and 1 Year, and tap the duration you want. Longer durations show the free months you get.
3. On the Set Staff Count step choose how many staff you need.
4. Review the Payment Summary, add a coupon if you have one, and tap Continue to Pay.
5. Complete the payment in the payment window. Your plan becomes active as soon as the payment is confirmed.
If you are still in the free trial, tap Buy a Plan to start this flow.

## How is the price of a plan calculated?

The price is: number of staff x price per staff per month x the months you pay for. The price per staff per month is the same on every duration of a plan. Longer durations include free months: a 6-month plan with 1 free month is charged for 5 months, and a 12-month plan with 2 free months is charged for 10 months. The plan still runs for the full 6 or 12 months. If you have an unused current plan, a credit is deducted (shown as Plan Upgrade Credit), then any coupon or referral discount is deducted, and 18% GST is added on top. The Payment Summary screen shows every line (Staff Charges, Plan Upgrade Credit, Subtotal, GST and Grand Total) before you pay. Prices per staff and the free months of each duration are shown in Plan Details.

## How do I renew my plan?

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity.
2. Tap Renew Plan.
3. Choose your staff count, review the summary and tap Continue to Pay.
Renewing extends your current plan by 12 months at the same plan level. The price per staff per month stays the same, and a renewal keeps the same share of free months as your plan has, so it can be charged for fewer than 12 months (for example 10 of 12 months). The Payment Summary shows exactly how many. If your plan is still active, the new period starts after your current Valid Until date, so you lose no days. If it has already expired, the new period starts today.

## Do I lose the remaining days if I renew early?

No. While your plan is still active, renewal adds 12 months starting from your current Valid Until date, so the days you have left are kept. Only after a plan has expired does the new period start from the day you pay.

## How do I add more staff seats in the middle of my plan?

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity.
2. Tap Upgrade Plan.
3. Choose the new, higher staff count and continue to the Payment Summary.
4. Pay to confirm.
Only the extra staff are charged, and your Valid Until date does not change. Coupons and plan credit are not used for this type of upgrade.

## How much will adding extra staff cost?

You pay only for the additional staff, not the ones you already have. The charge is extra staff x price per staff per month x the full calendar months left in your plan (at least 1 month), plus 18% GST. Free months do not apply to extra staff: you pay the monthly price for every month left, even if your plan includes free months. The exact amount is shown on the Payment Summary before you pay.

## Can I upgrade or renew during the free trial?

Upgrade Plan and Renew Plan are locked while the free trial is running. The app shows Not Available During Free Trial with the date the trial ends. You can still tap Buy a Plan to purchase a plan whenever you are ready; you do not have to wait for the trial to end.

## How do I switch to a different plan?

Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity > Plan Details, pick the plan and the duration you want and go through the checkout. If your current plan is still running, a credit for the unused part is applied automatically and shown as Plan Upgrade Credit in the Payment Summary. The credit is worked out from what your current plan cost, so its free months are not counted. For special cases, ask the team in Support > Live Chat.

## Why does the app say Downgrade Not Allowed?

The app does not let you move from your current plan to a lower plan on your own. It also does not let you pick the same plan with the same or a shorter duration than you have now, or a higher plan with a shorter duration. Choose a longer duration instead, or write to our team in Support > Live Chat and they will guide you.

## Why does the app say You're on This Plan?

You picked the plan you already have. To extend it, use Renew Plan. To add more staff, use Upgrade Plan. To move to another plan, pick a different plan in Plan Details.

## Which features are included in my plan?

Features depend on your plan level. During the free trial everything is available. On paid plans, some features such as CRM and staff location tracking are only available on higher plan levels, while the rest work on every active plan. Open Plan Details to compare plans before you buy. If a feature you need is locked, upgrade or contact Support > Live Chat.

## Why can I not open CRM or staff location tracking?

These two features are not part of every plan. Lower plan levels do not include them, and they are also blocked when your plan has expired. Check Settings > Your Plan & Validity to see your plan, and open Plan Details to see which plan includes them. Switching to a higher plan unlocks them.

## Why does the app say my staff limit is reached?

Your plan allows a fixed number of active staff, and you already have that many. The message looks like Staff limit reached with your count out of your limit. To add more people, open Settings > Your Plan & Validity > Upgrade Plan and buy extra seats. You can also deactivate staff who no longer work with you to free a seat.

## How many staff can I choose when I buy a plan?

On the Set Staff Count step you choose the number of staff with the plus and minus buttons. It must be a whole number of at least 1. The price scales with the number of staff you choose, so you can start with the number you need today and add more later with Upgrade Plan.

## Is GST added to the plan price?

Yes. 18% GST is added to the amount after discounts. The Payment Summary shows the GST amount and the Grand Total you will pay, and your invoice shows the same GST details.

## Can I buy or renew a plan on an iPhone?

On iPhone and iPad the app only shows your plan status (current plan and Valid Till date) and does not offer buying, renewing or upgrading. To purchase, use Clockyn on an Android phone or the web version, or ask the team in Support > Live Chat.

## Can I get a custom plan or a bigger discount?

Plan prices and offers are decided by the Clockyn team, so we cannot quote them here. On the Payment Summary there is an option to email the team asking for a better discount coupon. You can also chat with us in Support > Live Chat.

## Which plan durations can I choose, and how do free months work?

Core, Prime and Elite each come in several durations, for example 1 Month, 6 Months and 1 Year. More durations may be added later. In Plan Details, scroll sideways under a plan to see every duration with its price and what you save.
The price per staff per month is the same on every duration. Longer durations include free months: for example a 6-month plan can be charged as 5 months and a 1-year plan as 10 months, and you still get the full time. Each duration card shows its offer, such as 1 month free, and the saving per staff.

## What happens to my credit when I move to a longer plan?

When you move to another plan or a longer duration while your current plan is still running, a credit is applied and shown as Plan Upgrade Credit on the Payment Summary. The credit is worked out from what your current plan cost, counting only the months that were charged, not its free months. It is deducted from the new plan's staff charge before any coupon or referral discount and before GST.

## Do extra staff get the free months?

No. When you add staff with Upgrade Plan, the extra staff pay the normal monthly price for every full calendar month left in your plan, even if your plan includes free months. Your Valid Until date does not change. The exact amount is shown on the Payment Summary before you pay.
