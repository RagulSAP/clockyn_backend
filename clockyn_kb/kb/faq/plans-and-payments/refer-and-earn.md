## What is Refer & Earn?

Refer & Earn lets you invite other companies to Clockyn. You share your own referral code. When a new company enters it while registering, both companies get a discount on a plan: the company you referred gets the discount on its first plan, and you get the same discount on your next plan or renewal once they have paid for their first plan.
The discount percent is set by Clockyn and is shown on the Refer & Earn screen. The reward works on Prime and Elite plans only. Refer & Earn is available to the company admin.

## Where do I find my referral code and how do I share it?

1. Open Settings and tap Refer & Earn. It is the row right after Your Plan & Validity.
2. Your 6-character code is shown in large letters at the top. Tap the code or Copy code to copy it.
3. Tap Share to use your phone's share sheet, WhatsApp to send it in WhatsApp, or SMS to send it as a text message. Copy message copies a ready-made invitation that includes your code.
Every company has one code and it does not change.

## How does the company I refer use my code?

The new company types your code in the optional Referral code box on the register screen, when it creates its Clockyn account. When the code is right, the screen shows Referred by followed by your company name and the discount it will get.
The code can only be entered while registering. It cannot be added to an account that already exists, a company cannot use its own code, and a company can be referred only once. If the code is wrong, the register screen shows a message and asks for the correct code.

## How much do I save, and when can I use my reward?

Each referral gives a discount to both companies. The percent is shown at the top of the Refer & Earn screen, and again on the Payment Summary when you apply the reward.
A reward keeps the percent it had when it was issued. If Clockyn changes the percent later, only new referrals get the new percent.
The company you referred can use its reward right away, on its first paid plan after the free trial.
Your reward shows as Pending until that company pays for its first plan. Then it changes to Available, and you can use it on your next plan or renewal. Rewards from a friend's code do not expire.
The discount is taken off the staff charge before GST, after any plan credit.

## Which plans and purchases does the referral reward work on?

It works only on Prime and Elite plans, when you take a first plan after the free trial or renew your plan. It does not work on the Core plan, and it does not work when you add more staff with Upgrade Plan.
If you pick a plan it does not cover, the Apply button is greyed out and the card says why.

## How do I use my referral reward when I pay?

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity and choose your plan (or renew), then continue to the Payment Summary.
2. Under the coupon box you will see a Referral reward card with its discount percent and the number of rewards you have available.
3. Tap Apply. The card turns green and the price breakdown shows Referral reward with its percent and your new Grand Total.
4. Tap Pay as usual. To change your mind before paying, tap Remove.
The reward is used up only when the payment goes through. On iPhone the app only shows your plan status, so do this on an Android phone or the web version.

## Can I use a referral reward and a coupon together?

No. A purchase can use either a coupon or a referral reward, not both. This is true for a reward from a friend's code and for a reward from a partner promo code. If you apply one while the other is applied, the first one is removed and the app tells you. Choose whichever saves you more.

## I referred more than one company. Can I use the reward more than once?

Yes. Every company you refer gives you one reward, and you can use one reward per purchase. If you referred 2 companies, you can use a reward on two separate purchases, for example on two renewals. Each reward is available once the company you referred has paid for its first plan, and it has the percent that applied when that company signed up.

## Where can I see who I referred, and why can I not apply my reward?

Open Settings > Refer & Earn. Under Organisations you referred, each company shows its Joined date, a Signed up or Paid label, and the status of your reward: Pending (unlocks when they pay), Available, Used (with the plan and the amount saved) or Void. Your rewards show the Available, Pending and Used counts.
If you cannot apply a reward, check these: the reward is still Pending because the company has not paid yet, you picked the Core plan, you are adding staff with Upgrade Plan, or you already applied a coupon. If none of these fits, ask in Support > Live Chat.

## What is a partner promo code and where do I enter it?

A partner promo code is a code from a Clockyn partner, for example a YouTube, Instagram or Facebook channel that recommends Clockyn. It looks like a friend's referral code: 6 letters and digits.
Enter it in the optional Referral code box on the register screen when you create your company account. When the code is right, the screen shows Referred by, then the channel name and the discount, for example 15% off Prime and Elite.
The code can only be entered while registering. It cannot be added to a company that already exists. The partner never sees your company's data.

## How much discount does a partner promo code give, and how long is it valid?

The discount percent is set for each code, so different partners can give different discounts. You see the percent on the register screen when you enter the code, and again on Settings > Refer & Earn and on the Payment Summary.
It works like a friend's reward: once, on your first plan after the free trial or on a renewal, and on Prime and Elite plans only.
A code can have a validity period. If it does, your discount is valid for a set number of days after you sign up, and Settings > Refer & Earn shows Valid until with the date. After that date the reward shows as Expired and cannot be used. If no date is shown, the reward does not expire.

## Why does my referral or promo code say it is not valid?

The register screen shows This referral code is not valid when the code cannot be used. Check these:
1. A code has exactly 6 characters, letters and digits only, with no spaces. Capital and small letters both work.
2. Look-alike characters are easy to mix up, for example the digit 0 and the letter O, or the digit 1 and the letter I.
3. The code may have been switched off by the partner or by Clockyn.
4. The code can only be entered while registering, not after your company is created.
If the code is still refused, ask the person who gave it to you to confirm it, or write to us in Support > Live Chat.

## Why was my promo code reward not applied, or why does it show Expired?

A partner promo code reward is used on the Payment Summary like a friend's reward. If you cannot apply it, check these:
1. It has expired. Settings > Refer & Earn shows Expired when the validity days after your sign-up have passed. An expired reward cannot be used.
2. You picked the Core plan. The reward works on Prime and Elite only.
3. You are adding staff with Upgrade Plan. The reward works on a first plan or a renewal only.
4. You already applied a coupon. Use either a coupon or a reward, not both.
5. The reward was already used on an earlier payment.
The reward card on the Payment Summary says which one applies. If your reward expired before you could buy and you think it should still work, write to us in Support > Live Chat with your code and your sign-up date.

## Can I use a friend's referral code and a partner promo code together?

No. The Referral code box on the register screen takes one code, and a code cannot be added or changed after your company is created. Decide which code to use before you register.
Your own referral code is separate: after you register you can still share it, and you get a reward when a company you refer pays for its first plan.

## How can my business or channel become a Clockyn partner?

If you run a YouTube, Instagram or Facebook channel, or have another audience, and want to recommend Clockyn, write to us. Open the Support tab and send a message in Live Chat, or raise a ticket from Settings > Beyond > Support, with your channel name, its link and your contact details. Our team will explain the partner programme and, if you are accepted, give you your own code.
Every company that registers with your code gets a discount on Prime or Elite. We never share a company's data with partners.
