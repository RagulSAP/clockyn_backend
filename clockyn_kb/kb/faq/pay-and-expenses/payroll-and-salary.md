## How is a staff member's monthly salary calculated?

Net salary = Total Earnings minus Total Deductions.
Earnings: Basic Salary, House Rent Allowance, daily wages, hourly pay, incentives, bonus, overtime, odometer allowance, approved travel tickets and bills, and any extra earnings you added to the payslip.
Deductions: EPF, Professional Tax, Health Insurance, advances given in that month, a late fine (if you saved an Attendance Rule) and any extra deductions you added.
To see every figure, open Settings > View & Download Reports > Salary Reports > View Staff Salary.

## How do I create a salary structure (payslip) for monthly fixed staff?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Monthly Fixed.
2. Tap Add.
3. Enter the Payslip Name, Basic Salary and House Rent Allowance (all three are required).
4. Fill EPF, Professional Tax and Health Insurance if they apply. These are optional.
5. Use Add Earning or Add Deduction for any extra lines.
6. Tap Save Payslip. You will see 'Payslip Added!'.
The payslip only affects salaries after you assign it to staff.

## How do I assign a payslip to my staff?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Monthly Fixed.
2. On the payslip card, tap the three-dot menu and choose Assign Staff.
3. Select the staff members.
4. Tap Assign (the button shows how many you picked, for example Assign 3 Staff).
The card then shows 'View N Assigned Staff' so you can check who is on it.

## Can one staff member be on two payslips at the same time?

No. A staff member can have only one monthly payslip. If you try to assign a second one, Clockyn tells you 'Already assigned to' followed by the name of the current payslip.
To move the person, open Assign Staff on the old payslip, tap Remove next to their name, and then assign the new payslip.

## Which payslip fields are compulsory?

Payslip Name, Basic Salary and House Rent Allowance are required. EPF, Professional Tax and Health Insurance are optional; leave them blank if they do not apply and they count as zero.
Extra earning or deduction lines (Add Earning, Add Deduction) only count when you fill both a name and an amount.

## What is the maximum amount I can enter in salary fields?

Money amounts can be up to 9,999,999.99 with at most two decimal places. Percentages (such as Bonus or Turnover incentive) must be between 0 and 100, also with at most two decimals. Counts and minutes must be whole numbers.
If a value is not accepted you will see a message such as 'Must be a valid number' or 'Enter a number between 0-100'. Correct the value and save again.

## How do I edit a payslip I already created?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Monthly Fixed.
2. Tap the three-dot menu on the payslip and choose Edit.
3. Change the values and tap Update Payslip. You will see 'Payslip Updated!'.
The new figures apply to every staff member on that payslip for months that are not marked as paid yet. Months already marked as paid keep their saved figures.

## Why can't I delete an old payslip?

In Settings > Salary Structure > Monthly Fixed the Delete option is offered only on the newest payslip in the list. Older payslips show Edit and Assign Staff only.
When you delete the newest one, Clockyn asks 'Delete this template?' before removing it. If you need an older payslip changed, use Edit instead.

## What does 'A template with this name already exists' mean?

Every payslip in your organization needs its own name. This message appears when you save a payslip using a name that is already taken. Choose a different Payslip Name (for example add the grade or year) and tap Save Payslip again.

## How do I set up daily wages?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Daily Wages.
2. Tap Add and enter the amount under 'If one day present'.
3. Save. The template is named Day Pay 1, Day Pay 2 and so on.
4. Tap Assign Staff and pick the staff who are paid by the day.
Pay for the month = that amount x the number of days the person was present. Each amount can be used in only one Day Pay template, and assigning someone here moves them off their other Day Pay template.

## How are present days counted for daily wages?

A day counts as present when the staff member has at least one punch-in on that date. A day with two sessions still counts as one present day.
Daily wages for the month = Day Pay amount x present days. You can check each person's present days from Staff Details > Calendar or the attendance reports.

## How do I pay staff by the hour?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Hourly Calculation (the screen is titled Hourly Charges).
2. Tap Add and enter the amount under 'If one hour present'. The template is named Hour-Pay-1, Hour-Pay-2 and so on.
3. Tap Assign Staff and select the staff.
Hourly pay = that amount x the total hours of the staff member's completed sessions in the month (sessions with both a punch-in and a punch-out).

## Why is a staff member's hourly pay lower than I expected?

Hourly pay counts only completed sessions, meaning sessions that have both a punch-in and a punch-out. Hours of a day where the person forgot to punch out are not added until the session is closed.
Open Staff Details > Day-Wise to see the punches for each day and check for missing punch-outs.

## What types of incentives are available?

Open Settings > Salary Structure > Incentive / OT / Bonus > Incentive. The 'Select Incentive Type' screen offers two kinds:
- Count Basis: a fixed Each Incentive amount.
- Turnover Basis: a percentage of the Basic Salary.
Overtime and Bonus have their own entries in the same group. After creating an incentive, use Assign Staff to choose who receives it.

## How does the Count Basis incentive work?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Incentive / OT / Bonus > Incentive > Count Basis.
2. Tap Add and fill Count Upto Basic Salary, Next Count and Each Incentive.
3. Save, then tap Assign Staff.
Every month, the Each Incentive amount of the template is added to the earnings of each assigned staff member. You can see the result in View Staff Salary.

## How does the Turnover Basis incentive work?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Incentive / OT / Bonus > Incentive > Turnover Basis.
2. Tap Add and enter Turnover Upto Basic Salary and After Target Incentive (a percentage).
3. Save and use Assign Staff.
For each assigned staff member the incentive = Basic Salary x After Target Incentive % / 100, added to the month's earnings. It shows in the Turnover (₹) column of View Staff Salary.

## Why do I get 'This value already exists' when saving an incentive?

Each value must be different from the values in your other templates of the same kind. For example 'This value already exists in Count 1' means another Count template already uses that number; 'This percentage already exists in Bonus 2' means a Bonus template uses that percentage.
Change the value, or edit the existing template instead of creating a new one.

## How do I set up overtime pay?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Incentive / OT / Bonus > Overtime.
2. Tap Add, enter the duration in hours and minutes (minutes 0-59) and the Amount.
3. Save and tap Assign Staff.
For every full block of that duration worked beyond the shift, the amount is paid. The staff member must have a shift assigned so Clockyn knows when overtime starts. Each duration can be used in only one template.

## How is overtime calculated? Can you show an example?

For the month Clockyn adds up the hours of completed sessions and subtracts the shift hours for every day present. What is left is overtime. Overtime pay = number of full duration blocks x the template amount.
Example: shift 9 hours, present 20 days, worked 190 hours. Extra time = 190 - 180 = 10 hours = 600 minutes. With a template of 30 minutes = ₹100, pay = 20 blocks x ₹100 = ₹2,000. Partial blocks are not paid.

## Why is overtime not showing for a staff member?

Check these points:
1. The staff member is assigned to an Overtime template (Settings > Salary Structure > Incentive / OT / Bonus > Overtime > Assign Staff).
2. They have a shift assigned, otherwise Clockyn cannot tell how long a normal day is.
3. They worked more than the shift in total for the month; a short day is balanced against longer days.
4. Their sessions are closed (punched in and out). Extra time must also fill at least one full block of the template duration.

## How do I give a bonus to my staff?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Incentive / OT / Bonus > Bonus.
2. Tap Add and enter the percentage (0-100), the Month and the Year.
3. Save and use Assign Staff (the dialog is titled Staff Details). If someone already has another bonus, Clockyn warns you before selecting them.
Bonus = Basic Salary x percentage / 100, paid only in the month and year you chose.

## Why is the bonus not appearing in a staff member's salary?

A bonus is added only when all of these are true:
1. The staff member is assigned to the Bonus template.
2. You are looking at the same month and year that you picked in the template. Other months show no bonus.
3. The staff member has a payslip with a Basic Salary, because the bonus is a percentage of it.

## How can I charge a fine for late punch-ins? The Fine / Deduction menu says Coming soon.

The Fine / Deduction menu is not available yet, but late fines already work through the Attendance Rule.
1. Open Settings > Attendance & Devices > Attendance Rule.
2. Set Late Punch-In Count (1 to 10) and Consider As (Half Day or Leave).
3. Tap Save Rule.
For every group of that many late punch-ins in a month, one penalty applies: a Half Day counts 0.5 day and Leave counts 1 day. The fine shows under Fine (₹) in the salary.

## How is the late fine amount calculated?

Fine = penalty days x Basic Salary / working days of the month.
Example: the rule is 3 late punch-ins = Half Day. A staff member is late 7 times, so 2 penalties = 1 day. With Basic Salary ₹30,000 and 26 working days, fine = 1 x 30,000 / 26 = ₹1,153.85.
No fine is charged if you have not saved an Attendance Rule, or if the staff member has no monthly payslip.

## How do I add a travel allowance or any extra earning to a payslip?

1. Open Settings > Salary Structure > Monthly Fixed and edit the payslip (or tap Add for a new one).
2. Under earnings tap Add Earning, then type the name (for example Travel Allowance) and the amount.
3. For recurring deductions tap Add Deduction and do the same.
4. Tap Update Payslip or Save Payslip.
A line is counted only when both name and amount are filled.

## Where can I see the salary of all my staff for a month?

Open Settings > View & Download Reports > Salary Reports.
- View Staff Salary (titled Staff Salary) lists every component per person: Basic, Daily Wages, Hourly Calc, Count, Turnover, OT, Bonus, Fine, Odometer, Ticket, Other Bills and Advance.
- Monthly Salary shows who is paid or unpaid and the totals.
Use the month and year picker to change the month.

## How do I mark salary as paid?

1. Open Settings > View & Download Reports > Salary Reports > Monthly Salary.
2. Choose the month and year.
3. Tap Pay beside one person, or tick several people and tap Pay Selected, or tap Pay All.
4. Confirm 'Mark salaries as paid?' with Mark as paid.
You will see 'Payment Processed!' and the staff show a Paid badge. Paid people cannot be selected again.

## Does Mark as paid transfer money to my staff?

No. Mark as paid only records in Clockyn that you have paid the salary for that month, so the list, the totals and the payslip show it as Paid. The actual bank transfer or cash payment is done by you outside the app.

## What happens to a salary after it is marked as paid?

Clockyn saves the net amount and a copy of the full calculation. From then on the salary list, the breakdown and the payslip show those saved figures with a Paid status, even if you later change attendance, advances or templates.
Marking the same person again does nothing, so a saved payment is never overwritten.

## Can I undo a salary that was marked as paid by mistake?

The app has no option to unmark a paid salary, and a saved payment cannot be overwritten. Please double-check the figures in View Staff Salary before you tap Mark as paid. If you already marked one by mistake, contact Support > Live Chat and our team will help.

## How many staff can I mark as paid at once?

Up to 500 people in one go. You must select at least one person, otherwise Clockyn says 'Select at least one employee'. If your team is larger, mark them in two or more rounds with Pay Selected. If the salary of even one selected person cannot be calculated, nobody in that batch is marked.

## What do Total Amt, Unpaid Amt and Payment Progress mean on the Monthly Salary screen?

The cards at the top summarise the selected month:
- Total: number of staff in the list
- Paid and Unpaid: how many are paid or still pending
- Total Amt: the sum of net salaries
- Unpaid Amt: the net salary still to be paid
Payment Progress shows the percentage of staff already marked paid.

## Who appears in the Monthly Salary list?

All your active managers and staff appear in the list, each with their salary type, basic salary and net salary for the month. The admin account itself is not listed. Staff you have deactivated are not included in new salary lists.

## Can I see or pay the salary of a previous month?

Yes. Use the month and year picker on Monthly Salary or View Staff Salary to move to an earlier month. Unpaid months are recalculated from the attendance, advances and expenses of that month. Months already marked as paid show the saved figures.

## Does the salary change during the month?

Yes, until you mark it as paid. The figures follow the current attendance, approved expenses and advances of the month, so they grow as the month goes on. Once a month is marked as Paid, the saved amount no longer changes.

## How do I download the salary report?

1. Open Settings > View & Download Reports > Salary Reports > View Staff Salary.
2. Pick the month and year.
3. Tap Export.
4. Choose Download as PDF, Download as DOC or Download as Excel.
Previously downloaded files are kept in the report history.

## How can a staff member see their own payslip?

1. Open the Attendance Calendar from Home.
2. Tap Payslip.
The payslip shows the company name, Earnings, Deductions, the net pay in figures and in words. If you have not assigned a payslip to the person yet, it says 'No payslip assigned yet.' Assign one from Settings > Salary Structure > Monthly Fixed.

## Why is a staff member's net salary lower than their basic salary?

Deductions are taken from earnings. Check these items in View Staff Salary:
- EPF, Professional Tax and Health Insurance from the payslip
- Advance given in that month
- Fine for late punch-ins (needs an Attendance Rule)
- Any extra deduction lines on the payslip
Also note that days without punches do not earn daily or hourly pay.

## How do I remove a staff member from a wage, incentive or bonus template?

1. Open the template (Daily Wages, Hourly Calculation, Incentive, Overtime or Bonus) under Settings > Salary Structure.
2. Tap Assign Staff on its card.
3. In the list of assigned staff tap Remove beside the name.
The person stops receiving that amount from the next calculation of any month that is not paid yet.
