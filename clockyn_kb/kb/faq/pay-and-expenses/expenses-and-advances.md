## How do my employees submit expenses?

On the staff Home screen they open the Expenses tile and choose one of three cards:
- ODO Meter Reading: daily vehicle readings
- Travel Tickets: travel tickets and transport bills
- Other Bills: invoices and miscellaneous expenses
Every submission goes to you for approval. You review them under Settings > Expenses & Allowances.

## How does a staff member record an odometer reading?

Start of trip:
1. Open Expenses > ODO Meter Reading.
2. Take a photo of the odometer and enter Odometer Reading (KM).
3. Tap Save Start Reading. You will see 'Start Recorded!'.
End of trip:
4. Open the same screen, take a photo, enter the stop KM and tap Complete Reading. You will see 'Trip Complete!'.
Clockyn works out the distance for you.

## Can a staff member record more than one odometer trip in a day?

No. Clockyn keeps one odometer reading per day. After a start reading is saved for a date it must be completed with a stop reading, and saving a second start for the same date is refused with 'Start reading already submitted for this date'. For several trips, enter the first start KM and the final stop KM of the day.

## Why do I see 'Stop reading must be greater than start reading'?

The stop KM must be higher than the start KM saved earlier for that day. Equal readings are not accepted. Check that you typed the figure from the odometer correctly and did not swap start and stop. If the start reading was wrong, contact Support > Live Chat.

## What does 'Please upload image and enter KM value' mean?

The odometer screen needs both a photo of the meter and the reading. Tap Take Photo to capture it, type the kilometres in Odometer Reading (KM), then save again. KM can have up to two decimals and the stop reading cannot be lower than the start.

## How do I submit a travel ticket?

1. Open Expenses > Travel Tickets.
2. Check the date at the top. You can move back to earlier days but not into the future.
3. Take a photo of the ticket.
4. Enter the Amount (more than 0, up to 2 decimals).
5. Tap Submit Ticket.
You will see 'Ticket Submitted!' and the ticket appears in the list with the day's total.

## What is the difference between Travel Tickets and Other Bills?

Travel Tickets is for travel tickets and transport bills. Other Bills is for invoices and any miscellaneous expense. Both work the same way: a photo plus an amount, then approval by you.
In salary they appear in separate columns, Ticket and Other Bills, so choose the right one.

## How do I submit an Other Bill?

1. Open Expenses > Other Bills.
2. Select the bill date.
3. Tap to take a photo of the bill.
4. Enter the Amount.
5. Tap Submit Bill. You will see 'Bill Submitted!'.
If you tap Submit without a photo or amount, Clockyn says 'Please add a photo and enter amount'.

## The expense was saved but the photo failed to upload. What now?

Clockyn shows 'Saved, but the photo failed to upload'. The expense record is kept, so do not submit it again. Check your internet connection and tap Retry photo upload; this uploads only the photo for the same record, so no duplicate is created.

## The camera does not open when I try to add an expense photo.

Clockyn shows 'Camera permission is required' when the camera is blocked. Allow the camera for Clockyn in your phone settings (or see Settings > Beyond > App Permissions), then try again. You can use Retake to replace a photo or Remove to delete it before submitting.

## Where do I approve or reject expense claims?

Open Settings > Expenses & Allowances and choose Odometer, Travel Ticket or Other Bills. Each record shows the employee, date, amount and photo. Tap Approve or Reject and confirm. These buttons appear only while a claim is pending. You can filter by date and see how many records are shown.

## Can I change my decision after approving or rejecting an expense?

No. Clockyn asks you to confirm and warns 'This cannot be changed later.' Approve or reject is final. Open the photos first (the Photo or Photos button) and check the amount before you decide.

## How do approved expenses reach the salary?

Approved odometer readings, travel tickets and other bills are added to the staff member's earnings for the month of the reading or expense date. They show in the Odometer, Ticket and Other Bills columns of View Staff Salary. Pending and rejected claims are not added.

## How is the odometer allowance calculated and how do I change the rate per km?

Allowance = distance travelled x rate per km. Until you set a rate the app shows ₹3 per km.
To change it:
1. Open Settings > Expenses & Allowances > Odometer.
2. Tap edit on the rate card (Edit Allowance Rate).
3. Enter Rate per KM (₹) and tap Save Rate. The app saves whole rupees only.
The new rate applies to readings still pending; approved and rejected readings keep the rate they had.

## What should I do if an employee entered a wrong odometer reading?

Open Settings > Expenses & Allowances > Odometer and look at the Odometer Photos of that day (Start Reading and Stop Reading). If the figures do not match the photos, tap Reject while the claim is pending so the distance is not paid. Then ask the employee to contact you. Approval is final, so check before approving.

## How do I give a salary advance to a staff member?

1. Open the staff list from Home and tap the staff member to see Staff Details.
2. Open the Advance tab and choose Add Advance.
3. Pick the Date (it cannot be in the future) and enter the Advance Amount (₹), more than 0.
4. Tap Save Advance.
You will see a success pop-up, and the record is listed under View Advance.

## How is an advance deducted from salary?

The total of advances dated within a month is deducted from that month's salary, shown in the Advance (₹) column and as the Advance deduction on the payslip. For example, an advance of ₹5,000 dated 12 March is deducted in the March salary. Choose the advance date carefully because it decides the month.

## Who can give advances, and to whom?

Only the admin can give an advance. It can be given to managers and staff of your organization; the admin account cannot receive one. Other users who try get the message 'Only admin (role_id = 1) can assign advances'.

## What do 'Advance date cannot be in the future' and 'Amount must be greater than 0' mean?

The advance form checks two things. The date must be today or earlier, so pick a past or current date. The amount must be a number above zero with at most two decimals, up to 9,999,999.99. Fix the field and tap Save Advance again.

## Can I edit or delete an advance after saving it?

Clockyn does not show an option to edit or delete a saved advance. Each record in View Advance carries a Paid badge and the name of who added it. If an advance was entered by mistake, contact Support > Live Chat before the month's salary is marked as paid.

## Where can a staff member see the advances they received?

1. Open the Attendance Calendar from Home.
2. Tap Advance Salary.
The Bonus / Advance sheet lists each advance with its amount, date and who added it. If there are none you see 'No bonus or advance records yet.'

## What is the difference between an advance and an expense claim?

An advance is money you give to a staff member in advance; it is entered by you and deducted from their salary. An expense claim (odometer, travel ticket or other bill) is money the employee spent for work; they submit it with a photo, you approve it, and it is added to their salary. Advance is a deduction, an expense is an earning.
