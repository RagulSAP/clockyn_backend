## How do I view my company details?

Open Settings > Company Settings > View Company. You see your Contact Information (mobile, e-mail, website, address, state, district, pincode), Legal & Compliance details, and the number of Branches and Departments. The Edit Company button is at the bottom.

## How do I edit my company details?

1. Open Settings > Company Settings > View Company.
2. Tap Edit Company.
3. Change what you need, such as name, address, GST or logo.
4. Tap Update Company.
You will see Company Updated! when it is saved.

## How do I add another company?

Option 1: Settings > Company Settings > Add Company.
Option 2: on Home tap the switch company icon, then Add New Company.
Fill in the same details as at registration (name, address, state, district, pincode and at least one branch) and tap Add Company. You will see Company Added Successfully!

## How do I switch between my companies?

On Home, tap the switch company icon (up and down arrows) next to the company name. The Switch Company list shows all your companies with the current one marked. Tap another company and the app reloads with that company's staff, branches and settings.

## Can I run more than one company with one login?

Yes. Add more companies with Settings > Company Settings > Add Company and switch between them from Home. Each company has its own branches, departments, staff and shifts. The staff limit is checked for the company you are currently in.

## How do I delete a company?

1. Switch to the company you want to remove.
2. Open Settings > Company Settings > View Company.
3. Tap Delete and confirm. The app warns that this action cannot be undone.
The Delete button only appears when you own more than one company. After deleting, the app moves you to another company.

## Why can't I delete my company?

You cannot delete your only company ("Cannot delete the only company"), so the Delete button is hidden on View Company until you have added another one. If you want your account removed completely, use Request Account Deletion under More Options.

## What happens to my staff and branches if I delete a company?

Deleting a company also removes its staff accounts, branches, departments, shifts and additional details. This cannot be undone, so think twice. If you only want people to stop using the app, deactivate them instead of deleting the company.

## How do I upload or change my company logo?

1. Open Settings > Company Settings > View Company > Edit Company.
2. In the Company section tap Choose image (or Change if you already have one).
3. Pick the picture and tap Update Company.
Use Remove to take the logo off. If the logo upload fails, your other changes are still saved and the app tells you to try again from Edit Company.

## Is the GST number mandatory?

No, GST Number is optional. If you enter one it must be 15 characters in the usual GST format, for example 22AAAAA0000A1Z5. You can add or change it later in Edit Company.

## What are the Additional Details (PAN, CIN, TAN, MSME)?

They are optional business details. In Edit Company tap Add More Details to open PAN Number, Company Website, CIN Number, TAN Number, MSME Number and Business Category. They appear under Legal & Compliance in View Company. The app shows a format hint if a number looks wrong, but it does not stop you from saving.

## What format should PAN, CIN and TAN be in?

PAN: 10 characters, 5 letters, 4 digits, 1 letter (for example ABCDE1234F). TAN: 10 characters, 4 letters, 5 digits, 1 letter. CIN: 21 characters starting with L or U. Letters are converted to capitals as you type. A wrong format only shows a hint such as Invalid PAN format.

## What is Max Daily Punch Sessions?

It is how many times a staff member can punch in during one day. The default is 3, and you can set any number from 1 to 10. A session is one punch-in with its punch-out. You set it in Edit Company under Attendance Settings.

## How do I change the number of punch-ins allowed per day?

1. Open Settings > Company Settings > View Company > Edit Company.
2. Scroll to Attendance Settings.
3. Change Max Daily Punch Sessions (1 to 10).
4. Tap Update Company.
It applies to everyone in that company.

## How do I change the company mobile number or e-mail?

Open Edit Company and change the number or e-mail under Contact. A changed e-mail must be verified with a one-time code before you can save (the app says Please verify new email address). If your set-up also uses a mobile number, that one is verified the same way.

## How do I add a branch?

1. On Home open Manage > Branches and tap Add Branch (or use Add Branch on the card).
2. Enter Branch Name and Branch Address.
3. Tap the GPS Location box to locate the branch on the map and Save Location.
4. Choose the Attendance Radius.
5. Tap Add Branch.
Name, address and GPS location are all required.

## What is the attendance radius of a branch?

It is how close a staff member must be to the branch to punch in. Choose 100, 250, 500, 750 or 1000 metres; the default is 100 m. Outside it the app says how far away you are, for example "You are 250m away from your branch. Must be within 100m to punch." Field staff are not limited by it.

## How do I set or change a branch's GPS location?

Open Home > Manage > Branches, tap the pencil on the branch, then tap the GPS Location box. Stand at the branch and let the app find your position, or move the pin on the map, then tap Save Location (Update Location if one is already set). Finish with Update Branch.

## Why does it say "Branch name already exists"?

Each branch in a company needs its own name. Pick a different name, for example add the city or area. Check the Branch list first, because the name may already be used by an existing branch.

## How do I delete a branch?

1. Open Home > Manage > Branches.
2. Tap the trash icon on the branch.
3. Confirm Delete Branch (this cannot be undone).
If staff are still assigned to it, the app first asks you to move them with Reassign Staff.

## Can I delete a branch that still has staff?

Not directly. A branch with staff cannot be deleted until they move. The app shows "N staff in this branch must be reassigned before deletion". Select the staff (or Select All), pick another branch and tap Reassign All & Delete Branch, or Reassign to move only some of them first.

## How do I add a department?

1. On Home open Manage > Departments and tap Add Dept.
2. Type the Department Name (for example Sales, HR, Engineering).
3. Tap Save Department.
You can also list departments while adding a company. Department names must be unique inside the company.

## What happens to staff when I delete a department?

The department is removed and the people in it are left without a department. Edit each of them and choose a new Department Name. Confirm the Delete Department prompt carefully, as it cannot be undone.

## Why does it say "Department name already exists"?

Department names must be unique in your company. Choose another name or edit the existing department instead. The same message appears when you rename a department to a name that another one already has.

## What is Assign Devices in Settings?

Settings > Attendance & Devices > Assign Devices opens the Add Devices screen with Add Fingerprint Device, Add Iris Device, Add Face Authentication Device and Add RF Tag Device. These are coming soon and only show a message for now. Staff punch today with face and location through the app.
