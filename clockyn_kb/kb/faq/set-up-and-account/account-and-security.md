## How do I sign in to the admin app?

1. Open the app and choose Admin Portal.
2. Enter your e-mail address and tap Get OTP.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your e-mail and tap Verify & Continue.
Some set-ups sign in with a mobile number instead; the sign-in screen shows which one to enter. Use Change email if you typed it wrongly.

## I did not get the OTP e-mail. What should I do?

1. Check that the e-mail address is typed correctly (tap Change email to fix it).
2. Look in your spam or junk folder.
3. Wait a little and request a new code with Get OTP.
If you see "We could not send the email right now. Please try again in a moment", just try again shortly. Codes can only be requested a limited number of times per hour, so avoid tapping repeatedly. If it keeps failing, message Support > Live Chat.

## How long is my OTP valid?

Each OTP is a 6-digit code that is valid for 5 minutes. After that you need to tap Get OTP again. Requesting a new code replaces the old one, so always use the latest code you received.

## What does "Too many incorrect OTP attempts" mean?

You entered a wrong code 5 times for the same OTP, so that code is locked. Wait for it to expire (it is valid for 5 minutes in total) and then request a new code. Until then the app will say "Please wait for the current OTP to expire before requesting a new one".

## Why does it say "Please wait seconds before requesting another code"?

To protect your account, a new code cannot be requested straight after the previous one. Normally you must wait about 30 seconds between requests. The message shows the exact seconds left. Wait, then tap Get OTP again.

## What does "Too many codes requested. Try again later." mean?

You asked for more codes than allowed within one hour; the usual limit is 5 codes per hour. Wait a while and try again, and use the newest code you received instead of requesting more. If you are locked out for long, contact Support > Live Chat.

## What does "Invalid or expired OTP" mean?

The code you entered is wrong, older than 5 minutes, or has been replaced by a newer code. Tap Get OTP to receive a fresh one and enter it carefully. After 5 wrong tries the code is locked until it expires.

## Do I have to enter an OTP every time I open the app?

No. After a successful sign-in the device is remembered for up to 90 days, so you open the app straight in. You are asked for a code again after 90 days, after you log out, or when you sign in on a new device.

## How do I log out of the app?

Admin: Settings > Beyond > Logout, then confirm Logout.
Managers and staff: Settings tab > Logout button, then Confirm Logout.
You will need a new one-time code to sign in again on that device.

## What happens when I log out? Is my data deleted?

Nothing is deleted. Logging out only signs this device out and forgets its trusted-device status, so the next sign-in needs an OTP. Your company, staff and attendance data stay as they are.

## Can I be signed in on more than one phone?

Admin accounts can be signed in on several devices at the same time. For managers and staff it depends on how your Clockyn is configured: if one device per person is enforced, a second phone is refused with "This account is active on another device. Request access from your admin." The person then sends a device change request.

## Why does it say "This is an employee account. Please use employee login portal"?

You chose Admin Portal but the e-mail belongs to a manager or staff member. Go back to the first screen and choose Employee Access. The opposite message, "This is an admin account. Please use admin login portal", appears when an admin picks Employee Access.

## A staff member sees "There is no user with this email address". Why?

Employee sign-in only works for people already added by an admin. Check that the e-mail they typed is exactly the one in their staff record (Staff > open the person > Edit). If it is wrong, correct it in Edit Staff. If the person was never added, use Add Staff first.

## A staff member sees "Your account is inactive". What should I do?

That person was deactivated. Open Staff, choose the Inactive filter, find them and switch Active on. The app then lets them sign in again. Note that reactivating counts as a new seat, so your staff limit must have room.

## What does "Your account has been deleted" mean?

The account was removed, for example after a permanent delete or an approved account deletion request. The person cannot sign in with it any more. For a new start, an admin registers again or an admin adds the staff member again. For details, contact Support > Live Chat.

## What does "This email address is linked to more than one account" mean?

The same e-mail is used by more than one account, which is not allowed for sign-in. Ask your admin to give each person a unique e-mail. Admins can fix a staff e-mail in Edit Staff. If you are the admin, contact Support > Live Chat.

## Registration says "Email already registered". What now?

That e-mail already has an admin account. Go back to the first screen, choose Admin Portal and sign in with it instead of registering. If you do not remember registering, use a different e-mail or contact Support > Live Chat.

## How does a staff member ask to use a new phone?

When they sign in on a new phone the app says a different device was detected and offers Request Device Change.
1. Enter a Reason for Device Change (for example lost phone).
2. Tap Submit Request.
Their admin or manager approves it under Requests > Device Change. After approval the person signs in again with an OTP on the new phone.

## How do I approve a device change request?

Open Home > Quick Actions > Requests, then Device Change. Review the reason and approve or reject. On approval the old phone is signed out and the person can sign in on the new phone with an OTP. A rejection keeps things as they are.

## How many device change requests can one person send?

Only one request can be pending at a time ("A device change request is already pending"), and a person can send at most 3 requests in 24 hours. After that the app says "Too many device change requests. Please try again later."

## Where can I read the privacy policy?

Admin: Settings > Beyond > More Options > Privacy Policy. Managers and staff: Settings tab > More Options > Privacy Policy. The page opens inside the app. Tap I Understand, Go Back to return.

## How do I delete my account?

1. Open Settings > Beyond > More Options > Privacy Policy.
2. Tap Request Account Deletion.
3. Confirm with Submit Request.
The request is sent for review, and once reviewed all your data is permanently deleted. Right after you submit, the app signs you out.

## What happens right after I request account deletion?

The app shows a confirmation, marks your session inactive and signs you out after a moment. If you send the request again while it is still waiting, you get "Deletion request already submitted. Awaiting admin review." Nothing is removed until the request has been reviewed.

## Can I cancel an account deletion request?

The app has no cancel button for it. If you changed your mind, send a message in Support > Live Chat as soon as possible.

## Can I change the e-mail I use to sign in?

Admin: Settings > Company Settings > View Company > Edit Company, then change the e-mail under Contact. A changed e-mail must be verified with a one-time code before saving.
Staff: the admin changes it in Staff > open the person > Edit. The new e-mail must not be used by another account.

## How can a staff member change their profile photo?

1. Open the Settings tab > Profile Settings.
2. Tap Edit.
3. Tap the photo and choose Take Photo or Choose from Gallery.
4. Tap Save Changes.
Admins can also set a photo for a staff member in Edit Staff with Upload Photo or Change Photo.
