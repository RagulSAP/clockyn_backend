## How do I add a new staff member?

1. On Home open Manage > Staff and tap Add Staff.
2. Fill in Full Name, Employee ID, Phone Number, Email Address, Branch Name, Department Name and Gender.
3. Optionally pick a Shift and switch on Field Staff.
4. Add a photo or More details if you wish.
5. Tap Add Staff.
The staff member can then sign in with Employee Access.

## Which fields are required when adding staff?

Required: Full Name, Employee ID, Phone Number, Branch Name, Department Name and Gender. Email Address is also required when your Clockyn signs in by e-mail, because staff need it to sign in. Everything under More details (date of birth, blood group, emergency contact, address and bank details) is optional.

## How is the Employee ID chosen?

The app suggests the next free Employee ID when you open Add Staff, and you can type your own. Each ID must be unique in your company, otherwise you see "Employee ID already exists". Plain numbers are counted up automatically until an unused one is found.

## Why does it say "Employee ID, mobile number, or email already exists"?

Another person already uses the same Employee ID, phone number or e-mail. Check the staff list (search by name) and change the value you typed. Each staff e-mail must be unique, because it is how they sign in; the app may also say "This email address is already used by another account".

## How many staff can I add?

While on the free trial or without an active plan, up to 10 active managers and staff. With a paid plan the limit is the one in your plan. The Add Staff screen shows it as "Staff: X / Y" with the slots remaining. The admin account itself is not counted.

## It says "Staff limit reached". What can I do?

You have used all seats of your plan. Tap Upgrade on the red banner (on iOS it says View Status) or open Settings > Your Plan & Validity to change your plan. You can also free a seat by deactivating someone who has left. For plan options, ask in Support > Live Chat.

## Do inactive staff count towards my staff limit?

No. Only active managers and staff are counted. Deactivating someone frees their seat. Switching them back to Active takes a seat again and is checked against the limit, so it fails with Staff limit reached if you are full.

## How do I find a staff member?

Open Home > Manage > View Staff. Type in the search box (Search by name, branch, dept...) and use the chips All, Staff, Manager or Inactive to narrow the list. Tap a person to open their Staff Details.

## How do I edit a staff member's details?

1. Open the staff list and tap the person.
2. Tap Edit (on the card) or Profile > Edit Staff.
3. Change the fields.
4. Tap Update Staff.
If you leave with unsaved changes, the app asks whether to Save, Exit or Stay.

## How do I change a staff member's branch or department?

Open the person, tap Edit, change Branch Name or Department Name and tap Update Staff. To move many people at once when closing a branch, use the Reassign Staff step shown while deleting that branch.

## How do I deactivate a staff member?

Open the person's Staff Details and switch Active off, or use the switch in the staff list. Confirm Deactivate. The app says they will no longer be able to punch in, and you can reactivate them later.

## How do I reactivate a staff member?

Open Home > Manage > View Staff, choose the Inactive chip, find the person and switch Active on. The staff limit is checked again, so you need a free seat. After that they can sign in again.

## What is the difference between deactivating and deleting a staff member?

Deactivate is reversible: the person cannot punch in or sign in, and you can switch them back on. Permanent delete removes the person and all their records (attendance, salary, requests, chat and photos) and cannot be undone. Use deactivate for people who may return.

## How do I permanently delete a staff member?

Only the organization admin can do this.
1. Open the person's Staff Details.
2. Tap the trash icon (Delete staff permanently).
3. Type the person's name to confirm.
4. Tap Delete.
All their records are removed for good, so be sure.

## Why can't I delete a manager?

A manager who still has staff reporting to them cannot be deleted. The app says how many staff still report to them and asks you to reassign those staff to another manager first. Alternatively use Settings > Managers to remove the manager role, which turns them back into normal staff.

## Who can permanently delete staff?

Only the organization admin (the owner account). Managers and staff do not see the delete option, and the admin account itself can never be deleted this way.

## How do I make someone a manager?

1. Open Settings > Managers and tap Assign Manager (or +).
2. Optionally filter by Branch and Department, then choose the Staff Member.
3. Under Assign To pick at least one branch and one department.
4. Tap Assign Manager.
Then set what they may do with the permissions button.

## What can a manager do? What are the manager permissions?

You choose per manager from 10 permissions: Staff Management, Branch Management, Attendance Reports, Salary Management, Holiday Management, Shift Management, Leads Management, Incentive Management, Company Settings and Requests Management. A manager sees a shortcut in Settings only for the permissions you switched on.

## How do I change a manager's permissions?

1. Open Settings > Managers.
2. Tap the shield icon on the manager.
3. Switch permissions on or off (Select All is available).
4. Tap Save Permissions.
The sheet shows how many permissions are active, for example 3/10.

## How do I remove a manager?

Open Settings > Managers, tap the trash icon on the manager and confirm Remove. The person becomes normal staff again and their branch and department assignments are removed. They stay in your staff list.

## How do I see which branches and staff a manager looks after?

On Settings > Managers use View Branch on the manager's card to see the assigned branches with their departments, and the people icon to see the staff under that manager. The top shows Total managers, Assigned and Assignments.

## Why does a manager need a branch and a department?

Managers only work within the branches and departments you assign. The Manager form asks for at least one of each ("A manager needs at least one branch and one department"). Pick every department to cover a whole branch. An Access Preview shows the combinations.

## What is a field staff member?

Field staff work outside the office, for example sales or delivery. They can punch from anywhere, so the branch attendance radius does not apply to them. Switch on Field Staff in Add Staff or Edit Staff ("Enable if this employee works in the field").

## How do I assign a shift to a staff member?

In Add Staff or Edit Staff use the Shift field and pick a shift; its start and end time are shown. Need a new one? Tap + Add Shift to open Shift Setting. From a person's Staff Details you can also tap Assign and choose Shift.

## What does the Assign button on the staff screen do?

Assign opens a sheet. Settings group: Manager and Shift. Salary group: Monthly Fixed, Daily Wages, Hourly Charges, Incentive, Overtime (OT) and Bonus. Tap one to open that setup screen.

## What can I see on a staff member's detail screen?

Staff Details has the person's card (Profile, Assign, Edit) and five tabs: Overview (details and month snapshot), Calendar, Day-Wise, Location and Advance. The Active switch is on Overview.

## How can I add bank details for a staff member?

In Add Staff or Edit Staff tap Add More Details and fill Bank Name, Bank Branch, Account Number and IFSC Code. Date of Birth, Blood Group, Emergency Contact Number and Address are there too. The saved values appear under Profile > Bank Details.

## How does a staff member register their face?

The first time a staff member punches, the app opens face registration.
1. Allow the camera.
2. Take 4 photos: look straight, tilt slightly left, tilt slightly right, look straight again.
3. Tap Register Face.
4. Tap Get Started when it says registered.
After that, punches are matched against this face.

## Face registration keeps failing. What can I tell my staff?

Tell them to:
1. Face a light source and avoid strong light behind them.
2. Remove the mask, and glasses if they cause glare.
3. Hold the phone at eye level, at arm's length.
4. Fill the oval with their face and keep still.
The app also says things like too dark, hold the phone steady or move closer. If it still fails, contact Support > Live Chat.

## Can a staff member register their face again?

The app has no Re-register button in Settings or on the staff screen. If a face is not recognised, first follow the photo tips (good light, no mask, steady phone). If it still does not work, message Support > Live Chat and the team can help.

## What do managers see in their Settings tab?

Managers see an Admin Settings block with shortcuts only for the permissions you granted, for example Add Staff and View Staff, Add Branch and View Branch, Attendance Reports, Holiday Management or Requests Management. Below are General Settings: Profile Settings, App Permissions, Appearance and More Options. Staff see only the General Settings.

## How can I upload a photo for a staff member?

In Add Staff or Edit Staff use Upload Photo (Change Photo once one exists) and pick a picture. Remove clears it. Tap the photo on the staff list to view it larger. Staff can also change their own photo under Settings > Profile Settings.
