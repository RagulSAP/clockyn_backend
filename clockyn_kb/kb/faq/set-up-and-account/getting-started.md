## How do I register my company on Clockyn?

Open the app and choose Admin Portal. Enter your e-mail address, tap Get OTP and enter the 6-digit code from your inbox. If this e-mail has no account yet, the app takes you straight to Register Company.
1. Fill in Company Name, Address, State, District and Pincode.
2. Add at least one Branch (you can add departments too).
3. Verify your contact details with a one-time code.
4. Tap Register Company.

## What information do I need to register my company?

Required: Company Name, Company Address, State, District, a 6-digit Pincode and at least one Branch. Your contact details are verified with a one-time code.
Optional: GST Number (15 characters) and, under Add More Details, PAN, Website, CIN, TAN, MSME Number and Business Category. Departments are optional too. Everything optional can be added later from Settings > Company Settings > View Company > Edit Company.

## Is there a free trial when I sign up?

Yes. A new company starts with a free trial of 1 month from the day you register. During the trial you can add up to 10 active managers and staff. The trial end date and your plan status are shown under Settings > Your Plan & Validity, and the Home screen warns you with a Free Trial Ending Soon message before it ends.
For prices and plan details, see Your Plan & Validity or ask in Support > Live Chat.

## What should I set up first after registering?

A good order for a new company:
1. Check Settings > Company Settings > View Company and add your logo.
2. Add your branches with GPS location and radius (Home > Manage > Branches).
3. Add departments if you need more than the default ones.
4. Create shifts (Settings > Attendance & Devices > Shift Setting).
5. Add your staff (Home > Manage > Staff > Add Staff).
6. Set the Attendance Rule and holidays.
Staff can then sign in and punch with their face.

## What is the difference between Admin Portal and Employee Access?

Admin Portal is for the company owner or administrator: it opens the dashboard, staff, reports and settings. Employee Access is for managers and staff: it opens their own attendance, requests and personal records.
Use the portal that matches the account. If you pick the wrong one, the app tells you to use the other portal.

## What are the roles in Clockyn: admin, manager and staff?

Admin: owns the company and can use every screen, including plans, company settings and managers.
Manager: a staff member you promote. A manager sees only the Admin Settings shortcuts you have allowed through permissions, for the branches and departments you assigned.
Staff: punches attendance, sends requests and views their own records. Staff never see admin shortcuts.

## What do the tabs at the bottom of the app do?

As an admin you see five tabs:
1. Home: today's staff overview, filters and quick actions.
2. CRM: leads.
3. Chat: direct chats, groups and announcements.
4. Settings: company, salary, attendance, managers, expenses, appearance and more.
5. Support: FAQ, Live Chat and Video Hub.
Managers and staff see Home, CRM, Chat and Settings.

## What does the Home screen show?

Home shows Staff Overview for the selected date with these figures: Total Staff, Absent, Late Punched-In, Punched-In, Working and Punched Out. Above it are the Branch, Date and Department filters. Below are Manage cards (Staff, Branches, Departments) and Quick Actions (Staff Location, View Reports, Requests with a pending count).
If your plan or trial is ending, a reminder pop-up appears here too.

## How do I see who is absent or late today?

On the Home tab, tap the Absent or Late Punched-In figure in Staff Overview. The Staff list opens with only those people. The same works for Total Staff, Punched-In, Working and Punched Out. Change the Date, Branch or Department filter at the top of Home first if you want a different day or group.

## How do I filter Home by branch, department or date?

At the top of Home use the three filters:
1. Branch: ALL or one branch.
2. Date: pick the day to look at.
3. Department: narrow the figures to one department.
The Staff Overview figures and the staff lists you open from them follow these filters.

## How do I quickly add staff, a branch or a department from Home?

On Home, scroll to the Manage section. Each card (Staff, Branches, Departments) has a view button (View Staff, View Branch, View Dept) and an add button (Add Staff, Add Branch, Add Dept). The card also shows how many you have, for example staff on roll.

## Can I use Clockyn on a computer or only on my phone?

Clockyn runs on Android, iOS and in a web browser. The screens adapt to the screen size, so on a larger screen some pages show two columns. Sign in the same way on every device with your e-mail and a one-time code. For face punch, the device needs a camera.

## How do I switch to dark mode?

Open Settings and find Appearance. Choose Light, Dark or System. System follows your phone or computer theme. Managers and staff have the same Appearance choice in their Settings tab.

## Where can I find all the settings?

Tap the Settings tab. The sections are: Your Plan & Validity, Company Settings, Salary Structure, Attendance & Devices, View & Download Reports, Managers, Expenses & Allowances, Appearance and Beyond. Beyond holds Suggest a Feature, Support, More Options, App Permissions and Logout.

## How do my staff start using Clockyn?

1. Add them in Home > Manage > Staff > Add Staff with a correct e-mail address.
2. Ask them to install the app and choose Employee Access.
3. They enter their e-mail, tap Get OTP and enter the code.
4. They allow Camera and Location when asked.
5. On the first punch they register their face with 4 photos.
Staff you have not added cannot sign in.
