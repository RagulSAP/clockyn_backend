## How do I start a chat with a staff member?

1. Open the Chat tab and tap Individual Chat.
2. In the search box type a name, branch or department.
3. Tap the person to open the conversation and type your message.
People you already talked to stay in the list. Staff can message colleagues of the same organization in the same way.

## Can I send photos or documents in chat?

Yes. In the message box tap the camera icon to take a photo, or the attach icon to choose Photo library or Document. While a file uploads the camera and attach buttons are disabled. The default file size limit is 25 MB. If a file fails to send you can retry or discard it.

## What do the tick marks next to my messages mean?

- Clock: the message is still sending
- Red alert icon: it failed to send (you can retry)
- One tick: sent
- Two ticks: read by the other person
The time of the message is shown beside the ticks.

## How do I delete a chat?

1. Open the conversation and tap the menu in the header.
2. Choose Delete Chat.
3. Confirm. Clockyn says 'This cannot be undone.'
The whole conversation with that person is removed for both people. To look for something first, use Search in the same menu.

## How do I view a staff member's contact details from chat?

Open the conversation, tap the person's name or the header menu and choose View Contact. The sheet shows Emp ID, Mobile, Email, Department, Branch and Role.

## How do I create a group chat?

1. Open Chat > Group Chat.
2. Tap Create Group.
3. Type the Group name (up to 50 characters) and select the members. At least one member is required.
4. Tap Create. You will see 'Group created!'.
Only the admin can create groups. Staff see their groups in the list once the admin adds them.

## How do I add more people to a group?

1. Open the group and tap the menu in the header.
2. Choose Add Staff.
3. Select the people (only active staff who are not in the group are listed) and tap Add.
Any member of the group can add staff. Use View Members to see everyone, with the group admin marked as Admin.

## Can I remove someone from a group or leave a group?

Clockyn currently has no Remove Member or Leave Group option. The group menu offers View Members, Add Staff and, for admin, Search and Chat Theme. If someone must be removed, contact Support > Live Chat.

## How do I change the colour theme of a group chat?

1. Open the group as admin and tap the header menu.
2. Choose Chat Theme.
3. Pick one of Default, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Lavender, Rose, Dark or Teal.
The theme is saved on your device for that group, so it only changes how the chat looks for you.

## Why does the Chat tab say 'Feature Unavailable' or 'Chat Unavailable'?

Chat is blocked when your organization's plan does not include it or has lapsed. Admin sees 'Feature Unavailable' with a Renew Plan button (View Plan Status on iPhone). Managers and staff see 'Chat Unavailable' and should ask the admin. Check Settings > Your Plan & Validity.

## How do I send an announcement to all my staff?

1. Open Chat > Announcement and stay on the Compose tab.
2. Enter the Title (up to 100 characters) and Message (up to 1000).
3. Under Send To keep All Staff.
4. Tap Send to All Staff.
You see 'All staff have been notified' and every active manager and staff member gets a notification.

## How do I send an announcement to only some people?

1. Open Chat > Announcement.
2. Write the Title and Message.
3. Under Send To choose Select Staff.
4. Search and tick the people in Select Staff, then tap Done.
5. Tap the Send button (for example Send to 5 Staff).
Use Edit to change the selection before sending.

## How do I send an announcement to one branch or department?

1. Open Chat > Branch & Dept.
2. Choose Branch or Department and then pick the one you want under Select Branch or Select Department.
3. Write the Title and Message.
4. Tap the send button, for example Send to Mumbai.
Only active managers and staff of that branch or department receive it.

## Why can't I send my announcement?

The send button stays disabled, or an error shows, until everything is filled:
- 'Title is required'
- 'Message is required'
- 'Select at least 1 staff member' (for Select Staff)
- 'Select a branch' or 'Select a department' (for Branch & Dept)
Fix the missing item and send again.

## How do my staff see announcements?

Staff get a notification. In the Chat tab they open Broadcast (announcements from admin) or Targeted Announcement (for their branch or department) and can search by text. Staff can read announcements but only the admin can send them.

## Can I edit or delete an announcement after sending it?

You can delete but not edit. Open the History tab of Announcement or Branch & Dept, find it with the search box and tap Delete. Confirm 'Delete Announcement?'. It is removed for good and staff no longer see it. To correct a mistake, delete it and send a new one.

## Who receives an announcement sent to All Staff?

All active managers and staff of your organization receive it, as a notification and in their Broadcast list. Staff you have deactivated do not. The admin account that sends it is not counted as a recipient.

## How do I see my past announcements?

Open Chat > Announcement (or Branch & Dept) and switch from Compose to History. The tab shows the count, for example History (12), with the most recent first. Each card shows who it was sent to. Search by title; in Branch & Dept you can also search by branch or department name.
