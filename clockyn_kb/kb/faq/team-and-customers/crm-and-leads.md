## What is the CRM tab used for?

CRM helps you collect client leads and hand them to your staff. As admin you add leads (Enter Leads), assign them (View / Assign Leads) and follow their progress under Work Status Overview and Carry Forward Requests. Staff and managers see their own tasks under My Leads and update the status as they work.

## How do I add a new lead?

1. Open CRM > Enter Leads.
2. Fill Name, Mobile, Address and Category. Remarks and the customer required date are optional.
3. Tap Save Lead.
If a field is missing you will see 'Please fill all required fields'. The mobile number must be exactly 10 digits.

## How do I create a new lead category?

1. While entering or editing a lead, tap the Category field to open Select Category.
2. Tap Add and type the name under Category name.
3. Tap Add again to save.
Category names are not case sensitive, so 'Category already exists' appears if the name is already in use. You can also search the list and delete a category you no longer need.

## How do I assign a lead to a staff member?

1. Open CRM > View / Assign Leads.
2. Pick the day with the date chips (Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow or Pick Date).
3. Tap Assign on the lead.
4. Filter by Branch or Department if needed, then tap Assign next to the employee.
A success pop-up confirms the assignment and the employee sees the lead in My Leads.

## Can a lead be assigned to more than one person?

A lead is handled by one employee at a time. Assigning the same lead to the same person again returns 'Lead is already assigned'. To change the person, tap Reassign on the lead; afterwards only the new employee is assigned.

## How do I edit or delete a lead?

In CRM > View / Assign Leads, each lead card has Edit, Delete and Assign buttons.
- Edit opens the lead form; tap Update Lead to save.
- Delete asks 'Are you sure you want to delete this lead?' and removes it for good.
Only leads that are not completed can be changed.

## Why are the Edit, Delete and Assign buttons disabled on a lead?

A lead marked Completed is locked. The card shows a Completed badge, Edit, Delete and Assign are disabled and tapping a locked lead says 'Completed leads cannot be assigned'. Completed work is kept as a record; if a client needs a new job, add a fresh lead.

## What do the numbers on the Work Status Overview mean?

Work Status Overview shows tiles for Total, Unassigned, Pending, Working, Completed and Carry Fwd leads for the selected day, followed by a Lead-wise Status list. Tap a tile to filter the list, for example Unassigned shows the leads that still need a staff member.

## What does the lead status Pending, Working, Completed or Carry Forward mean?

Status shows the progress of the employee's work on the lead:
- Pending: assigned, not started
- Working: the employee is on it
- Completed: finished (the lead is then locked for you)
- Carry Forward: the employee has asked to move it to another date and waits for your decision

## How do my staff see and update their leads?

Staff and managers open CRM > My Leads (Assigned to you). It lists the leads assigned to them that are due today or have no date. Filter with All, Pending, Working or Completed. Tap a lead to open the status sheet and choose a new status. Opening My Leads also clears the new-lead badge on the CRM tab.

## My employee says they cannot see an assigned lead. Why?

My Leads shows leads assigned to the employee that are due today or have no due date. A lead dated for another day appears on that day. Also ask them to pull down to refresh. If CRM is blocked by the plan they see a CRM Unavailable card instead. Check the lead's date and assignee in CRM > View / Assign Leads.

## What is a carry forward request?

When an employee cannot finish a lead today, they choose Carry Forward in the status sheet, write a Reason, pick the Forward to date (tomorrow by default) and tap Send Request. They see 'Request Sent!'. The request waits for you under CRM > Carry Forward Requests, where you approve or reject it.

## How do I approve or reject a carry forward request?

1. Open CRM and go to Carry Forward Requests. A red badge shows how many are pending.
2. Read the Employee, Reason, Requested for date and Submitted on.
3. Tap Approve (confirm 'Yes, Approve') or Reject.
Approval moves the lead to the requested date. Rejecting puts the lead back to the status it had before. A request can be actioned only once.

## Why can't my employee send a carry forward request?

Clockyn refuses a request in these cases:
- No reason written: 'Description is required'
- The forward date is in the past: 'forward_date cannot be in the past'
- A request for the same lead is already waiting: 'A pending carry forward request already exists'
- The lead is not assigned to that employee
Fix the reason or date, or wait for your decision on the earlier request.

## Can staff add leads themselves?

Yes. Staff and managers tap Add Lead on My Leads and fill the Add New Lead sheet (name, a 10-digit mobile, address and category, with optional remarks). They see 'Lead saved and sent to your admin for assignment'. The lead then appears as Unassigned for you in CRM > View / Assign Leads so you can assign it.

## What does the mobile number error on a lead mean?

The mobile number of a lead must contain exactly 10 digits. Clockyn shows 'Mobile number must be exactly 10 digits' (or '...must be 10 digits.' in the staff form). The field accepts digits only and stops at 10, so remove country codes, spaces or dashes.

## Why does CRM say 'CRM Unavailable'?

Your organization's plan does not currently include CRM, or the plan has expired. Admin sees a Renew Plan button (View Plan Status on iPhone) that opens Your Plan & Validity. Managers and staff only see the message and need to ask their admin. Check Settings > Your Plan & Validity.

## How can I search leads by date?

In CRM > View / Assign Leads use the date chips at the top: All, Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow, or Pick Date to choose any day. If nothing matches you see 'No leads found for this date'. The same date chips filter the Work Status Overview.
