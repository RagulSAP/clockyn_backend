## How does an employee punch in?

Employees punch in from the Home screen with face verification.
1. Open the Clockyn app and go to Home.
2. Tap the PUNCH IN button.
3. Allow Location and Camera access if the app asks.
4. Look at the camera, keep your face inside the oval and tap to capture.
After the face is verified you see 'Punched In!' with the time. The app first checks that the employee is inside the branch area (field staff are exempt).

## How does an employee punch out?

After punching in, the same big button on Home changes to PUNCH OUT and the status shows 'Currently Working'.
1. Tap PUNCH OUT.
2. Verify your face in the camera, the same way as for punch in.
You see 'Punched Out!' with the time. The employee must be inside the branch area when punching out, unless they are field staff.

## What do the punch button labels PUNCH IN, PUNCH OUT, CHECKING, SETUP FACE and LIMIT REACHED mean?

PUNCH IN: you are not punched in and can start work (status 'Not Punched In').
PUNCH OUT: you are working right now (status 'Currently Working').
CHECKING: the app is still loading your face registration or attendance, wait a moment.
SETUP FACE: your face is not registered yet (status 'Setup Required'), tap it to register.
LIMIT REACHED: you used all punch-in sessions allowed for today (status 'Max Sessions Reached'), the button is locked until tomorrow.

## What does LIMIT REACHED mean on the punch button?

LIMIT REACHED means the employee has already completed the maximum number of punch-in sessions allowed for today and is not punched in. The button is disabled until the next day. If it appears too early, the admin can raise the daily limit (see the question on daily punch sessions) or correct the day with Edit Attendance.

## How many times can an employee punch in per day?

Each punch in followed by a punch out is one session. If your company never changed the setting, an employee can have up to 3 sessions per day. The admin can change this from 1 to 10 in the company form under Settings > Company Settings, field 'Max Daily Punch Sessions'. After the limit is reached and the employee is punched out, the button shows LIMIT REACHED.

## How do I change the maximum daily punch sessions for my company?

1. Open Settings > Company Settings.
2. Open the company form (Add Company).
3. Find 'Max Daily Punch Sessions' and choose a value from 1 to 10.
4. Save.
The new limit applies to all employees of the company, and Edit Attendance by the admin also follows it. If you do not set one, 3 sessions per day are allowed.

## Why does the app ask me to set up my face before I can punch?

Clockyn marks attendance only by face verification, so every employee registers their face once. When someone taps the button for the first time it shows SETUP FACE and asks for Location and Camera permission. After a successful registration they can punch in and out with their face. Admins who want to punch also register their face the same way.

## How do I register my face?

1. Tap SETUP FACE on the Home screen and allow Camera and Location access.
2. Take 4 photos as instructed: look straight, tilt your head slightly left, tilt slightly right, then look straight again.
3. Review each preview ('Does this look clear?') and tap Register Face, or Retake if it is dark or blurry.
4. Wait for 'Registering your face...'.
You see 'Face Registered' when it works. Tap Get Started and you can punch.

## What are the tips for good face photos during registration?

Use a well-lit place and face the light. Look straight at the camera and keep your face centred in the frame. Remove masks, caps and glasses that cause glare. Keep the phone steady at about arm's length and take the photo when the preview looks sharp. Registration needs all 4 photos to show your face clearly, so retake any photo that looks dark, blurry or cropped.

## Face registration failed with 'Face not visible in some photos'. What should I do?

This means one or more of the 4 photos did not clearly show your face, and Clockyn does not save a registration with a bad photo. Move to a brighter place, face the light, remove glare-causing glasses or a mask, and fill the oval with your face. Then register again and use Retake on any preview that does not look clear. You get up to 3 tries per photo.

## Can an employee register or change their face again?

Face registration is a one-time setup that appears as SETUP FACE until it is done, and after that the button becomes PUNCH IN. If an employee's face is often not recognised, first follow the tips for a clear photo. If it still fails, for example after a big change in appearance, ask Support > Live Chat what can be done.

## Does the face I register also become my profile photo?

Yes. When the registration succeeds, Clockyn also saves the first photo as the employee's profile photo. This is done in the background, so if it fails for any reason the face registration itself still works and the employee can update their photo later.

## The app says 'Photo is not matching - please try again'. What does it mean?

The face in the camera did not match the face registered for this employee. Nothing is sent to the admin for review, and you simply try again. Tips: face a light source, avoid strong light behind you, remove mask or glare-causing glasses, hold the phone at eye level and fill the oval. Make sure the employee punching is the account owner, because punching for someone else will not work.

## What does 'We couldn't see your face' mean while punching?

The camera could not find a face in the photo. Fill the oval with your face, look straight at the camera and make sure nothing covers it. If the message repeats, check the lighting and wipe the camera lens, then tap to capture again.

## The camera says it is too dark or too blurry. What should I do?

For 'It's too dark - move to a brighter place', go near a window or a light and face it, not the other way round. For 'Hold the phone steady and try again', rest your elbows, keep still and capture again. If your face is too small the app says 'Move closer - fill the oval with your face'.

## I keep failing face punch. Is there any help in the app?

Yes. After every second failed attempt in a row the app shows 'Tips for a clear photo' with a 'Try again' button. The tips are: face a light source and avoid strong back light, remove mask and glasses that cause glare, hold the phone at eye level at arm's length, and fill the oval and keep still. If it still fails after that, contact Support > Live Chat.

## What happens when someone holds a photo or video in front of the camera?

Clockyn checks that the face is a real, live person. A printed photo or a screen is refused with 'Spoof detected. Please use a real face.' and attendance is not marked. The employee should try again with their real face in good light.

## I get 'More than one face detected' when I punch. What should I do?

Only one person may be in the frame. Step away from others, make sure no posters or screens show faces behind you, and capture again. Attendance is marked only when exactly one face is found.

## Why does the app say I am outside the branch area?

For employees who are not field staff, Clockyn checks that the phone is inside the radius of the branch before punching. If you are too far, the app shows 'Outside Branch Area' with the distance and the allowed distance, for example 'You are 250m away from your branch. Must be within 100m to punch.' Move closer to the office, wait for a good GPS signal and try again. The default radius is 100 metres.

## What is the punch radius of a branch?

It is the distance around the branch location within which employees can punch. If the branch has no special value set, Clockyn uses 100 metres. Employees outside the radius cannot punch in or out. If you see many 'Outside Branch Area' messages for people who are at the office, check the branch location and radius or ask Support > Live Chat.

## What does 'Location Required' or 'Location Error' mean when I punch?

'Location Required' means Location permission is off. Clockyn needs your location to check that you are inside your branch, so enable Location for the app in your phone Settings. 'Location Error' means the phone could not get your position, so check that GPS is on, step outside or near a window, and tap again.

## Why does the app ask for Camera and Location permission?

The camera is used to verify your face. The location is used to confirm that you are within your branch area when punching. Allow both in the pop-up or in your phone Settings. You can review them in Settings > Beyond > App Permissions. Without them the punch cannot be completed.

## Can field staff punch from anywhere?

Yes. If an employee is marked as Field Staff, the branch distance check is skipped and they can punch in and out from any place. To mark someone, switch on 'Field Staff' (Enable if this employee works in the field) in the staff form. Branches without a saved location also skip the distance check.

## How does location tracking for field staff work?

For employees marked as Field Staff, the app starts sharing their location after they punch in and stops after they punch out. The location is sent about every 20 seconds while they work. On Android it can continue in the background, on other devices the app should stay open. The admin watches it under Staff Location.

## Where can I see where my field staff are?

Open Staff Location from the Home quick actions. Choose the Branch and Date, then use the Map or Satellite view. Staff are split into those who updated within the last 15 minutes and those whose last update is older than 15 minutes. You can also open a staff member's details and use the Location tab for their route history.

## Why can't I see location for a staff member?

Location is recorded only for staff marked as Field Staff, and only between their punch in and punch out. If the person has not punched in, has already punched out, or the app has no location permission, nothing is shown. Also check that you picked the right date and branch on the Staff Location screen.

## Can someone punch in on a holiday?

It depends on how the holiday was created. If the holiday has 'Block Attendance' on, staff in that branch and department see 'Today is a holiday (name). Attendance is not required.' and cannot start a new punch in. If it is off, staff can still punch in. Staff who are already working can still punch out.

## I punched in but forgot to punch out. What happens?

If a session stays open for more than 18 hours, Clockyn treats it as a forgotten punch out. At the employee's next punch in, the old session is closed automatically at the shift end time (or 9 hours after the punch in when there is no shift) and flagged as auto-closed. The punch out button will not work on such an old session and says no active punch in was found. Admin can fix the exact time with Edit Attendance.

## What does it mean when attendance shows an auto-closed day?

It means the employee did not punch out and the system closed the session automatically. The hours are an estimate, so overtime for that day is skipped until it is reviewed. The admin can set the real punch out in Edit Attendance, which removes the auto-closed flag.

## Can an employee punch in twice without punching out?

No. If the employee is already punched in, the app shows PUNCH OUT instead. A direct attempt shows 'Already punched in. Please punch out first.' The employee needs to punch out before starting a new session. Likewise punch out needs an open punch in.

## I can't punch out and it says 'No active punch in found'. Why?

This appears when there is no open session to close. Typical reasons are that you have not punched in today, you already punched out, or your last punch in was more than 18 hours ago and was treated as forgotten. Check Home and your attendance calendar, and ask your admin to correct the day with Edit Attendance if needed.

## What do the Home status chips SETUP, WORKING, COMPLETE and NOT IN mean?

SETUP: face registration is pending. WORKING: the employee is punched in. COMPLETE: the day's work is done, with a punch in and punch out recorded or the daily session limit used. NOT IN: no punch in yet today. The chip changes immediately after each punch.

## I get 'Feature Unavailable' or 'Your plan has expired' when punching. Why?

Face punching is part of the company plan. If the plan has expired or does not include it, the app shows 'Feature Unavailable'. Only the admin can fix this. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity to check the status and renew, then the employees can punch again.

## The app shows 'Connection error' or 'Capture failed' while punching. What should I do?

'Connection error - check your network' means the photo could not reach the server, so check mobile data or Wi-Fi and try again. 'Capture failed - tap to retry' means the photo could not be taken, so tap again. For 'Camera error - close and retry', close the camera screen, open it again and make sure no other app is using the camera.

## What does 'The face service is busy' mean?

The verification service had too many requests at that moment. Wait a few seconds and try again. Your attendance is not marked until the face is verified successfully.

## Can an employee punch in from a different phone?

The face is checked on the server, so the employee can punch from the phone they log in with. Companies that use single-device login ask managers and staff to send a Device Change request from their account, which the admin approves. After approval, the old phone is signed out and the employee can log in on the new one.

## How can the admin fix a wrong or missing punch?

1. Open Staff from the dashboard and tap the employee.
2. Open the Day-Wise tab.
3. Tap the punch in or punch out time of the date.
4. In Edit Attendance set the hours and minutes and tap Save Changes.
You can edit any session up to the company's daily limit, and a session cannot be created before the earlier sessions of that day exist. If another session is still open, close it first.

## How do I see an employee's punch photos and times for a day?

Open Staff, tap the employee and use the Calendar or Day-Wise tab. Tapping a day opens Attendance Details with the Punch In and Punch Out times and photos. You can also open a person from the filtered staff list on the Home dashboard for a chosen date, to see their punch times and photos.

## How does an employee see their own attendance?

On the employee side, open the Attendance Calendar from the employee screens. Each day is coloured as Present, Absent, Leave, Half Day or Sunday, and tapping a day shows its punch details. Approved leave also appears on the calendar.

## What do the attendance day types Present, Late, Half Day, Absent, Leave, Holiday and Weekly Off mean?

Present: the employee punched in. Late: first punch in was after the shift start. Half Day: the employee worked less than half of the shift and has no open session. Absent: a working day in the past with no punch, leave or holiday. Leave: an approved leave date. Holiday and Weekly Off: no work expected. If several apply, punches win over leave, then holiday, then weekly off.

## When is an employee marked Late?

Clockyn looks at the first punch in of the day. If it is later than the shift start time, the day is Late. For night shifts, a punch up to 12 hours after the start time counts as late, so arriving early at 8 PM for a 9 PM shift is not late. Employees without an assigned shift cannot be late.

## When is a day counted as Half Day?

A day becomes Half Day when the employee worked at least a minute but less than half of the shift length, and has no session still open. If no shift is assigned, a 9 hour day is used as the base. This is separate from the Attendance Rule, which turns repeated late arrivals into a half day or leave.

## Which date does a night shift punch belong to?

A session that starts at night belongs to the date it started, even if the punch out is after midnight. So a 10 PM to 6 AM shift started on Monday is shown on Monday and not split over two days. Dates are based on Indian Standard Time.

## Which time zone does Clockyn use for attendance?

Attendance dates and punch times follow Indian Standard Time (IST). The date of a punch is its IST date. Late and half day decisions use IST as well.

## How do I mark someone present if they have no phone or could not punch?

Use Edit Attendance. Open Staff, tap the employee, go to the Day-Wise tab, tap the punch in or punch out time for that date, enter the times and tap Save Changes. A punch in or punch out time is required. The edit follows your company's daily session limit.
