## How do I create a shift?

1. Open Settings > Attendance & Devices > Shift Setting.
2. Tap Add New Shift.
3. Enter the Shift Name.
4. Pick the Start Time and End Time.
5. Tap Save.
The shift appears in the list with its duration, and you can then assign staff to it.

## Can I create a night shift that ends the next day?

Yes. Set the start time in the evening and the end time in the morning, for example 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM. Clockyn understands that the end is on the next day. Late and half day rules work for night shifts too, and the session is counted on the date it started.

## Why does the app say my shift works out to too many hours?

A new or edited shift cannot be longer than 18 hours. The message shows the computed hours and asks you to check the start and end time, especially AM or PM. A common cause is choosing PM instead of AM for the end time.

## How do I assign staff to a shift?

1. Open Settings > Attendance & Devices > Shift Setting.
2. Open the shift menu and choose Assign Staff.
3. Select one or more employees.
4. Confirm.
The staff are now on that shift ('Staff assigned!'). An employee has one shift at a time, so assigning moves them from their previous shift. To take someone off, use Remove on the shift.

## How do I remove an employee from a shift?

Open Settings > Attendance & Devices > Shift Setting, open the shift's staff list and tap Remove next to the employee. The app asks 'Remove name from this shift?'. After confirming, the employee has no shift assigned until you assign another one.

## What happens if I delete a shift?

The app asks you to confirm because the action cannot be undone. All staff who were on that shift are unassigned, and the message tells you how many. Assign them to another shift afterwards so late and half day rules keep working for them.

## Can I edit a shift's timing?

Yes. In Settings > Attendance & Devices > Shift Setting open the menu of the shift and choose Edit, change the name, start time or end time and save. The same limit of 18 hours applies. Staff stay on the shift.

## What happens if an employee has no shift?

Attendance still works, but there is no shift start to compare with, so the employee is never marked Late. For half day and overtime Clockyn uses a 9 hour working day as the base. We recommend assigning a shift to every employee. You can see who is unassigned in the Employee Shift / Timing report.

## How do I see which employees are on which shift?

Go to Settings > View & Download Reports > Employee Shift / Timing. Employees are grouped by shift with the shift timing and duration, and those without a shift appear under Unassigned. You can also open a shift in Shift Setting to see its assigned staff.

## How do I add a holiday?

1. Open Settings > Company Settings > Holiday Management.
2. Tap a date on the calendar.
3. Enter the Holiday Name, for example Diwali.
4. Choose the branch and department it applies to, or leave them for everyone.
5. Set Block Attendance on or off.
6. Tap Add Holiday.
The month list shows the count of holidays.

## What does Block Attendance mean on a holiday?

With Block Attendance on, staff cannot punch in on that day ('Staff cannot punch in on this day'). With it off, staff can still punch in if they choose ('Staff can still punch in if they choose'). New holidays have blocking on by default.

## Can I set a holiday for only one branch or department?

Yes. When adding a holiday choose a Branch and a Department. Leaving them as All means the whole company. Two holidays cannot share the same date and the same branch and department, and trying to add a duplicate is refused.

## How do I edit or delete a holiday?

Open Settings > Company Settings > Holiday Management and select the month. Open the holiday in the list to use Edit Holiday, where you can change the name, the branch and department, or Block Attendance. To remove it, choose Delete, and the app asks 'Delete Holiday?' before removing one date or all dates of that holiday. Deleting cannot be undone.

## How do I set weekly offs like Sunday or Saturday?

In Holiday Management tap Configure Off Days and select the days of the week that are off for your company. The list can include any days from Monday to Sunday. If you never set it, Sunday is the weekly off. Weekly offs are not counted as absent.

## What is the Attendance Rule and where do I find it?

The Attendance Rule decides what happens when an employee is late too many times. Open Settings > Attendance & Devices > Attendance Rule. Under Half Day Rule choose the Late Punch-In Count (1 to 10) and Consider As (Half Day or Leave), then tap Save Rule. A sentence below the fields summarises it, for example 'If an employee is late 3 times, it will be considered as Half Day.'

## How is the late penalty calculated from the Attendance Rule?

For each full set of late days, one penalty unit is counted. With the rule set to 3 times and Half Day, 3 late days make half a day and 6 late days make one day. If Consider As is Leave, each set counts as a full day. The penalty feeds into the salary as a deduction. Until you save a rule, no late penalty is applied.

## Is there a grace time for late punch in?

The Attendance Rule screen in the app does not offer a grace period setting. A punch in is Late when it is after the shift start time. If your company needs a grace period, ask the team through Support > Live Chat.

## How does overtime work in Clockyn?

Overtime is set up under Settings > Salary Structure > Incentive / OT / Bonus > Overtime. For each employee Clockyn adds the hours worked in the month and subtracts the shift hours for each present day. Anything above that is overtime, and it is paid per block of minutes that you define in the overtime setting. Days with an auto-closed session are skipped until reviewed. Check the Overtime Report for the figures.

## Why do two branches have different holidays on the same day?

Holidays can be set for the whole company or for a specific branch and department. Each employee gets the holidays that match their own branch and department plus the company-wide ones. Open Holiday Management and check the Branch and Department on each holiday.

## Which Settings menu has shifts, holidays and the attendance rule?

Shift Setting, Assign Devices and Attendance Rule are under Settings > Attendance & Devices. Holiday Management is under Settings > Company Settings. Reports are in Settings > View & Download Reports. Managers see only the parts they have permission for.

## Which permissions can I give a manager for attendance rules and shifts?

In Managers you can switch on permissions such as Shift Management, Holiday Management, Attendance Reports, Company Settings and Requests Management, then tap Save Permissions. Other permissions available are Staff Management, Branch Management, Salary Management, Leads Management and Incentive Management. The manager sees only the screens they are allowed to use.
