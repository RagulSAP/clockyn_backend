## What types of requests can employees raise?

From the Requests option employees can send four types: Leave Request, Permission Request, Shift Change Request and Device Change Request. Admins and managers see them all in one place, with a badge counting pending ones, and approve or reject each.

## How does an employee apply for leave?

1. Open Requests and choose Leave Request.
2. Tap the dates on the calendar, you can select several days.
3. Write the reason (maximum 500 characters).
4. Tap Submit Leave Request.
You see 'Leave request submitted successfully'. Leave dates cannot be in the past, and a request cannot include a date that already has a pending or approved leave.

## What is a permission request?

A permission request is for a short absence inside one day, such as leaving early or coming late. Pick the date, a start time and an end time, add a reason and tap Submit Permission Request. It can be at most 6 hours, the end time must be after the start time, and the date cannot be in the past.

## How do I request a shift change?

1. Open Requests and choose Shift Change Request.
2. Select the Preferred Shift from the list of company shifts.
3. Choose the Requested Shift Date.
4. Write the reason and tap Submit Request.
You cannot ask for the shift you are already on, the date cannot be in the past, and only one request per date is allowed. After approval the new shift applies for that day.

## What is a device change request and when do I need it?

It is used when an employee gets a new phone or loses theirs and the company allows one device per employee. The employee fills in the reason, such as 'Lost phone, damaged device, new phone', and submits. Once the admin approves, the old device is signed out and the employee can log in on the new one. Only one request can be pending, and at most 3 can be sent in 24 hours.

## How does the admin approve or reject a request?

1. Tap Requests in the Home quick actions.
2. Open the type, for example Leave Requests.
3. Check the employee, dates and reason on the card.
4. Tap Approve or Reject.
The employee gets a notification with the decision. You can change a decision later using 'Change to Approve' or 'Change to Reject'.

## Can I approve only some dates of a leave request?

Yes. Leave and permission requests are decided date by date. Approve or reject each date on the card, or use Approve and Reject to decide all dates at once. The request is shown as pending while any date is pending, approved if at least one date is approved, and rejected if all are rejected.

## What are the request statuses Pending, Approved and Rejected?

Pending means no decision yet. Approved means the admin or manager accepted it, and Rejected means it was declined. Employees see these in My Requests, and a notification arrives on every decision. For leave, the status of each date can differ.

## Does an approved leave change attendance?

Yes. An approved leave date shows as Leave on the attendance calendar instead of Absent. If the employee still punches in on that date, the punch takes priority and the day is shown as present. Rejected or pending dates are treated like normal days.

## Who can approve requests?

The admin can decide every request of the company. A manager can handle requests only when they have the Requests Management permission, and normally for staff in their assigned branches and departments. Give the permission in Managers and tap Save Permissions.

## What is the number on the Requests tile or badge?

It counts the requests that still need a decision, across leave, permission, shift change and device change. When you open a list, its new-request badge clears, and new requests raise it again. On the employee side the badge counts the employee's own pending requests.

## I see 'A leave request already exists for one of the selected dates'. Why?

One of the days you picked is already covered by a pending or approved leave request of yours. Open My Requests to find it, then choose only dates that are free. Rejected requests do not block a new one.

## Why can't I choose a past date for leave, permission or shift change?

Requests are for today or a future date only. The app shows messages such as 'Leave dates cannot be in the past'. For a past day, the admin can fix attendance directly using Edit Attendance.

## How do I see the status of my requests as an employee?

Open Requests and choose My Requests to see the leave, permission, shift change and device change requests you sent, with their status. New Request starts a new one. The list refreshes when you pull down, and a notification arrives when a request is decided.

## What is the 'Reason is required' or 'too long' error on requests?

A reason is needed for every request type. It must not be empty and can be up to 500 characters. Add a short clear reason and submit again.
