## Where do I find reports?

Open Settings > View & Download Reports, or tap View Reports in the Home quick actions. The Generate tab lists the report types, and the View Reports tab shows the ones you generated earlier. Reports are grouped as Attendance Reports, Salary Reports, Employee Reports and CRM.

## Which attendance reports are available?

Attendance Reports has Monthly Summary, Staff-wise Detailed Report, Late Report and Overtime Report. Employee Reports has Employee Details and Employee Shift / Timing. Salary Reports has Monthly Salary and View Staff Salary. Some rows such as Incentives, Loss of Pay and Previous Employees appear greyed out because they are not available yet.

## How do I generate the monthly attendance report?

1. Open Reports and choose Monthly Summary.
2. Pick the month and year.
3. Choose All staff or one employee.
4. Tap Generate & View PDF.
The PDF opens and is saved in View Reports for later.

## Can I download reports in Excel or CSV?

Yes. The Export Report sheet offers Download as PDF, DOC, Excel and CSV. The monthly report is generated as a PDF, and employee and other reports open the format list. Your earlier downloads appear in the View Reports tab.

## What does the Late Report show?

It lists each late day of the selected month with employee, branch, department, shift start, punch in time and how late they were ('Late By', for example 25 min or 1h 5m). When shift times are missing the shift and Late By cells show a dash. Choose the month at the top and export it if needed.

## What does the Overtime Report show?

For the month it shows each employee's overtime and the amount earned, based on the Overtime setting in Salary Structure. Employees without overtime appear in grey. Days with a forgotten punch out are left out until the admin corrects them.

## What is the Staff Detailed Report?

Staff-wise Detailed Report gives one row per employee for the chosen month with working days, present, absent, late, half day, leave, Sundays and the hours worked, along with branch and department. Select the month, check the figures on the screen and download it in your preferred format.

## What is in the Employee Details report?

Employee Details lists your employees with employee ID, department and branch. Tap an employee to see their profile report, then View it or Download it as a file in the format you choose.

## What does the Staff Overview on the Home dashboard show?

Staff Overview shows today's numbers as rings you can swipe: Total Staff, Absent, Late Punched-In, Punched-In, Working and Punched Out. Each ring shows its share of total staff. The carousel moves by itself and stops when you touch it.

## What is the difference between Punched-In, Working and Punched Out on the dashboard?

Punched-In counts staff who punched in on the chosen date. Working counts those who are punched in right now. Punched Out counts those who have finished and punched out. Absent counts staff without attendance, and Late Punched-In counts those whose first punch in was after the shift start. Tap any figure to see the people.

## How do I see the list of absent or late staff?

On the Home dashboard tap the Absent or Late Punched-In figure. The staff list opens already filtered, and you can also choose Total, Punched-In, Working or Punched Out. Tap a person to see their punch times and photos for that date.

## How do I check attendance of an earlier date or one branch?

Use the filter bar on the Home dashboard. Choose a Branch (or ALL), switch to Department view if you want, and pick a Date. Future dates cannot be selected. If a past date has no punches, the app shows 'No attendance record for this date'.

## How can a manager see reports?

Give the manager the Attendance Reports permission under Managers. The manager then finds a reports entry in their Settings. They can open Monthly Summary, Late Report, Monthly Salary and View Staff Salary, limited to their staff, while other reports stay admin-only.

## Where are my downloaded reports saved?

Open Reports and tap the View Reports tab. Every report you generated appears there with its type (PDF, Excel and so on) so you can open it again. If nothing is there the app says 'No reports yet' and asks you to use the Generate tab.

## Why is my report empty or failing to download?

Check that you picked the right month and that staff have attendance in it. If the screen shows an error, tap Retry. For a failed download the app says 'Failed to generate PDF' or 'Failed to download CSV'. Try again after checking your internet connection, and contact Support > Live Chat if it keeps failing.

## How do I see a staff member's monthly attendance calendar?

Open Staff, tap the employee and use the Calendar tab to see each day coloured by status for the month, or Day-Wise for a table of punch in, punch out and hours. Tap a day for the details and photos. You can change the month at the top.
