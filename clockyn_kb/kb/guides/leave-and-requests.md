Staff send requests from the **Requests** option in the app. There are four kinds: **Leave Request**, **Permission Request**, **Shift Change Request** and **Device Change Request**. Admins and managers see all of them in one place, decide each one, and the employee gets a notification. This guide covers leave, permission and shift change. For a new phone see [Device change requests](kb:device-change-requests).

## Apply for leave (staff)

1. Open **Requests** and choose **Leave Request**.
2. Tap the dates on the calendar. You can select several days.
3. Write the reason. It is required and can be up to 500 characters.
4. Tap **Submit Leave Request**.

You see "Leave request submitted successfully". The calendar only lets you pick today or a later day. Sundays are greyed out. A date that already has a request of yours cannot be picked again, even if that request was rejected. The app also refuses a date covered by a pending or approved leave: "A leave request already exists for one of the selected dates".

## Apply for permission (staff)

A permission request is for a short absence inside one day, such as leaving early or coming late.

1. Open **Requests** and choose **Permission Request**.
2. Pick the date.
3. Pick a **Start Time** and an **End Time**. Times are offered in 30-minute steps.
4. Write the reason.
5. Tap **Submit Permission Request**.

Rules: the date cannot be in the past, the end time must be after the start time, and it can be at most **6 hours**. On today's date the start time must be later than the current time.

## Ask for a different shift for one day (staff)

1. Open **Requests** and choose **Shift Change Request**.
2. Select the **Preferred Shift** from the shifts of your company.
3. Choose the **Requested Shift Date**.
4. Write the reason and tap **Submit Request**.

You cannot ask for the shift you are already on: "You are already on this shift". The date cannot be in the past, and a second pending request for the same date is refused. After approval the new shift applies **only on that date**. Every other day your assigned shift applies.

## See your own requests (staff)

Open **Requests** and choose **My Requests**. It lists the requests you sent with their status. **New Request** starts another one. Pull down to refresh. The badge on the Requests tile counts your own pending requests.

## Approve or reject (admin and managers)

Who can decide: the admin for every request of the company. A manager only with the **Requests Management** permission, normally for staff in the branches and departments assigned to that manager.

1. On **Home**, tap **Requests** in the Quick Actions. The screen is titled **Manage Staff Requests**.
2. Open the type, for example **Leave Requests**. The badge counts what still needs a decision.
3. Check the employee, dates and reason on the card.
4. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**.

The employee is notified of the decision. You can change a decision later with **Change to Approve** or **Change to Reject**. Leave and permission requests are decided **date by date**: approve or reject each date on the card, or use the main buttons to decide all dates at once.

## What the statuses mean

- **Pending:** no decision yet. A leave request stays pending while any date is pending.
- **Approved:** accepted. A leave request counts as approved if at least one date is approved.
- **Rejected:** declined. A leave request is rejected only when every date is rejected.

## What an approved leave does to attendance

An approved leave date shows as **Leave** instead of **Absent**. If the employee still punches in on that date, the punch wins and the day shows as present. Pending or rejected dates are treated like normal days.

## Common questions

### Why can I not pick a past date?
Requests are for today or a later date. For a past day, the admin can fix attendance with **Edit Attendance**, see [Attendance and face punch](kb:attendance-and-face-punch).

### Why can I not pick a Sunday?
The request calendar greys out Sundays.

### Can I approve only some days of a leave?
Yes. Decide each date on the card, or decide all dates together.

### What is the number on the Requests tile?
It counts requests that still need a decision. For staff it counts their own pending requests.

### Who can approve requests?
The admin, and managers who have the **Requests Management** permission. Give it in **Managers** and tap **Save Permissions**.

### How long can the reason be?
Up to 500 characters, and it cannot be empty.

## What can go wrong

- **"A leave request already exists for one of the selected dates":** one of your dates is already pending or approved. Open **My Requests** and pick free dates.
- **"Reason is required":** add a short reason and submit again.
- **"End time cannot be more than 6 hours after start time":** shorten the permission or split it.
- **"You are already on this shift":** choose a different preferred shift.
- **A manager cannot approve:** the manager lacks the **Requests Management** permission, or the employee is outside the manager's branches and departments.

## When to contact support

Contact Clockyn support through **Support > Live Chat** if a request does not reach the admin or an approved leave does not show on the calendar after you refreshed. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, the employee name, the request dates and a screenshot.
