All plan changes start in **Settings > Your Plan & Validity**. Only the company admin can buy, renew or change a plan. This guide covers each case: your first purchase, renewing, adding staff, and moving to another plan or a longer duration.

> **Note** On iPhone and iPad the app only shows your plan status and does not offer buying, renewing or upgrading. Use Clockyn on an Android phone or the web version to purchase.

## Buy a plan for the first time

1. Open **Settings > Your Plan & Validity**.
2. Tap **Plan Details**. Under each plan (Core, Prime, Elite), scroll sideways to see its durations, for example 1 Month, 6 Months and 1 Year. Tap the duration you want. Longer durations show the free months you get.
3. On the **Set Staff Count** step, choose how many staff you need.
4. Review the **Payment Summary**, add a coupon if you have one, and tap **Continue to Pay**.
5. Complete the payment in the payment window. Your plan becomes active as soon as the payment is confirmed.

If you are still in the free trial, tap **Buy a Plan** to start this flow.

## Renew your plan

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity.
2. Tap **Renew Plan**.
3. Choose your staff count, review the summary and tap **Continue to Pay**.

Renewing extends your current plan by **12 months** at the same plan level. The price per staff per month stays the same, and a renewal keeps the same share of free months as your plan has. So it can be charged for fewer than 12 months, for example 10 of 12 months. The Payment Summary shows exactly how many.

If your plan is still active, the new period starts after your current **Valid Until** date, so you lose no days. If it has already expired, the new period starts today.

> **Warning** The staff limit of your plan becomes the number you choose on the Set Staff Count step. When you renew, choose at least the number of active staff you have, so that nobody is locked out later.

## Add more staff seats during your plan

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity.
2. Tap **Upgrade Plan**.
3. Choose the new, higher staff count and continue to the Payment Summary.
4. Pay to confirm.

Only the extra staff are charged, for every full calendar month left in your plan (at least 1 month), plus 18% GST. **Free months do not apply to extra staff.** Your Valid Until date does not change. Coupons and plan credit are not used for this type of upgrade.

## Switch to a different plan or a longer duration

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity > **Plan Details**.
2. Pick the plan and the duration you want and go through the checkout.

If your current plan is still running, a credit is applied automatically and shown as **Plan Upgrade Credit** on the Payment Summary. The credit is based on what your current plan cost, counting only the months that were charged, not its free months. It is deducted before any coupon or referral discount and before GST.

## Why some options are blocked

- **Downgrade Not Allowed**: the app does not let you move to a lower plan on your own. It also does not let you pick the same plan with the same or a shorter duration than you have now, or a higher plan with a shorter duration. Choose a longer duration, or contact support.
- **You're on This Plan**: you picked the plan you already have. To extend it, use Renew Plan. To add staff, use Upgrade Plan. To move to another plan, pick a different one in Plan Details.
- **Not Available During Free Trial**: Upgrade Plan and Renew Plan are locked during the free trial. You can still tap Buy a Plan.

## Common questions

### Do I lose my remaining days if I renew early?
No. While your plan is still active, renewal adds 12 months starting from your current Valid Until date. Only after a plan has expired does the new period start from the day you pay.

### Can I use a coupon on a renewal?
Yes. On the Payment Summary you can use a coupon or a referral reward on a renewal or a plan change, but not both on the same purchase. Coupons are not available for the add-staff Upgrade Plan.

### How many staff can I choose?
You choose with the plus and minus buttons on the Set Staff Count step. The minimum is 10 staff when you buy your first plan or renew. If you already have a plan and switch to another one, you cannot go below your current staff limit.

### What happens to my credit if I move to a longer plan?
The credit is applied as Plan Upgrade Credit on the Payment Summary. For special cases, ask in Support > Live Chat.

### Where do I see my staff limit and how many I use?
Settings > Your Plan & Validity shows **Staff Limit**, **Used Staff** and **Remaining**.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Staff limit reached" | All seats of your plan are used | Use Upgrade Plan to add seats, or deactivate someone who left |
| Renew Plan or Upgrade Plan is locked | You are on the free trial, or on iPhone | Use Buy a Plan, or use an Android phone or the web version |
| Downgrade Not Allowed | The option you picked is lower or shorter than your current plan | Choose a longer duration or contact support |
| The plan did not change after payment | The payment is not confirmed yet | See [Payments and invoices](kb:payments-and-invoices) |

## When to contact support

If you want a custom arrangement, a downgrade, or something looks wrong on the Payment Summary, please contact Clockyn support in Support > Live Chat. Have these ready:

- Your company name and admin e-mail
- Your current plan, Valid Until date and staff limit
- What you want to change, and a screenshot of the Payment Summary
