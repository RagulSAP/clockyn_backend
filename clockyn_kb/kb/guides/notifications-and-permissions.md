Clockyn needs a few permissions on the phone to work properly: location, camera, notifications and photos. This guide explains why each one is needed, where to switch it on, which alerts the admin and the staff receive, and what to do when alerts do not arrive.

## Which permissions the app needs and why

| Permission | What it is used for |
| --- | --- |
| Location | GPS check-in and attendance tracking. It confirms that you are inside your branch when you punch. |
| Camera | Face recognition and photo capture. |
| Notifications | Alerts, reminders and updates. |
| Photos | Attaching images to requests, expenses and support tickets. |

Without Location and Camera a punch cannot be completed.

## Check or change permissions (admin)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Beyond**, then **App Permissions**.
3. You see a card for **Location**, **Camera**, **Notifications** and **Photos**, a summary such as "3 of 4 permissions granted", and a badge that says **All Active** or the number **Pending**.
4. Tap a card to allow it.

## Check or change permissions (staff and managers)

Staff and managers open their own **Settings** and tap **App Permissions**. Their screen is titled **App Permissions** with the line "Tap a permission to manage it". It shows **Location**, **Camera** and **Notifications**, each marked **Enabled** or **Disabled**.

## What happens when you tap a permission card

- If the permission was never asked, the phone shows its normal permission prompt.
- If you already allowed it, nothing needs to be done.
- If you blocked it earlier, the app opens your phone settings so you can switch it on. The status updates when you come back to the app.
- On Android, after you allow Location the app also asks for **Allow all the time** so attendance tracking for field staff can work in the background. iPhones do not ask for this step. If you refuse it, the app opens the phone settings.
- In a web browser the app cannot open the browser settings. It tells you to allow the permission for this site in your browser's site settings, usually from the lock icon next to the web address, and then reload the page.

## Notifications the admin receives

- A staff member or manager punches in or out. Tapping it opens the home screen. You are not alerted for your own punches.
- A staff member submits a request such as leave or a device change. Tapping it opens the requests screen.
- Someone sends you a chat message or a group message. Tapping it opens the conversation.
- The Clockyn support team replies in **Live Chat**. Tapping it opens **Support > Live Chat**, and the Live Chat row shows an unread badge.

## Notifications staff receive

- Announcements and new polls.
- New chat and group messages.
- The decision on their requests. Tapping it opens their requests screen.
- A resolved support ticket.

## When alerts do not arrive

Check these in order:

1. **Settings > Beyond > App Permissions**: **Notifications** should be allowed.
2. The phone itself must have notifications on for Clockyn, and **Do Not Disturb** or battery saver must not block them.
3. Check the internet connection.
4. Log out and log in again so the phone registers for alerts again.

## Turn notifications off

Switch **Notifications** off for Clockyn in the phone settings. The **App Permissions** screen shows the current status, but a blocked permission can only be changed in the phone's own settings. You may then miss punch alerts, requests and chat messages.

## Common questions

### Why does the app ask for my photos?
So you can attach images to requests, expenses and support tickets. If you deny it, the app asks you to allow photo library access when you try to pick a picture.

### Why did I not get an alert when staff punched in?
Punch alerts go to the phones of your admins. Notifications must be allowed, the phone must be online, and you must be logged in on that phone. You can always check punches on the home screen.

### Why does the camera not open when I punch?
The app checks the camera permission, then GPS, then your branch area, and only then opens the camera. Make sure **Camera** and **Location** are both allowed.

### Why does the app say "Allow location to punch in"?
Punching checks that you are inside your branch area, so location permission is needed first. Allow it in **App Permissions** and try again.

### Do staff get a notification when I approve their leave?
Yes. Staff are notified of every decision on their requests.

### Do Live Chat replies from support send a notification?
Yes, to organisation admins. Live Chat is only available to admins.

## What can go wrong

- **Permission stays Pending after tapping:** it was blocked earlier. The app opens the phone settings, so switch it on there and return to the app.
- **No alerts on a web browser:** allow notifications for the site in the browser's site settings and reload.
- **Alerts only arrive late:** battery saver or Do Not Disturb may be delaying them.

## When to contact support

Contact Clockyn support through **Support > Live Chat** if all permissions are on and alerts still do not arrive after you logged out and in again. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, the phone model and Android or iPhone version, and what you expected to receive.
