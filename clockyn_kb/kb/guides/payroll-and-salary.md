Clockyn works out each staff member's salary for a month from their salary setup, their attendance, approved expenses and advances. You can see the full breakdown, mark salaries as paid, and download the figures. This guide explains how the numbers are made. The company admin sets up and pays salaries. A manager sees salary reports only if the admin gave them the matching permission.

## How the monthly salary is calculated

On the Salary Reports screens in Clockyn, the net salary of a person for a month is:

**Net salary = Total Earnings minus Total Deductions**

Earnings added together:

- Basic Salary and House Rent Allowance from the person's monthly payslip
- Daily wages (if the person is on a Day Pay template)
- Hourly pay (if the person is on an hourly template)
- Incentives (Count Basis and Turnover Basis) and Bonus
- Overtime
- Odometer allowance, approved Travel Tickets and approved Other Bills
- Any extra earning lines you added to the payslip

Deductions subtracted:

- EPF, Professional Tax and Health Insurance from the payslip
- Advances given with a date in that month
- A late fine, only if you saved an Attendance Rule
- Any extra deduction lines you added to the payslip

If one person has more than one pay type assigned (for example a monthly payslip and an hourly template), Clockyn adds all of them. Only active managers and staff are calculated; the admin account is not part of salary.

## A worked example

Say a staff member is on a payslip with Basic Salary ₹30,000, House Rent Allowance ₹6,000, EPF ₹1,800 and Professional Tax ₹200, plus an extra earning line "Travel Allowance" ₹1,000. In March they also have a Count Basis incentive of ₹500, a Turnover Basis incentive of 2%, a Bonus of 10% set for March, and an advance of ₹5,000 dated in March.

- Turnover incentive = 30,000 x 2 / 100 = ₹600
- Bonus = 30,000 x 10 / 100 = ₹3,000
- Total Earnings = 30,000 + 6,000 + 1,000 + 500 + 600 + 3,000 = ₹41,100
- Total Deductions = 1,800 + 200 + 5,000 = ₹7,000
- Net salary = 41,100 - 7,000 = ₹34,100

If an Attendance Rule applied a late fine of ₹1,153.85 for that month, the deductions would be ₹8,153.85 and the net salary ₹32,946.15. See [late fines and deductions](kb:salary-structure-and-deductions).

## Daily wages and hourly pay

For daily wages, the pay for the month is the Day Pay amount multiplied by the days the person was present. A day counts as present when there is at least one punch-in on that date, and a day with two sessions still counts as one day. For example, ₹800 a day and 22 present days gives ₹17,600.

For hourly pay, the pay is the hourly amount multiplied by the total hours of completed sessions in the month. A completed session has both a punch-in and a punch-out. For example, ₹100 an hour and 180.5 hours gives ₹18,050.

## See the salary of all your staff for a month

1. Open Settings > View & Download Reports > Salary Reports.
2. Choose View Staff Salary (the screen is titled Staff Salary) to see every component per person: Basic, Daily Wages, Hourly Calc, Count, Turnover, OT, Bonus, Fine, Odometer, Ticket, Other Bills and Advance.
3. Or choose Monthly Salary to see who is paid or unpaid, with the totals.
4. Use the month and year picker to change the month.

On Monthly Salary the cards at the top show Total (number of staff), Paid and Unpaid counts, Total Amt (the sum of net salaries) and Unpaid Amt (the net salary still to be paid). Payment Progress is the percentage of staff already marked as paid.

## Mark a salary as paid

Mark as paid records in Clockyn that you have paid. It does not send money; you pay by bank transfer or cash yourself.

1. Open Settings > View & Download Reports > Salary Reports > Monthly Salary.
2. Choose the month and year.
3. Tap Pay beside one person, or tick several people and tap Pay Selected, or tap Pay All.
4. Confirm "Mark salaries as paid?" with Mark as paid.

You will see "Payment Processed!" and the staff show a Paid badge. You can mark up to 500 people in one go; for a bigger team, do it in rounds. If the salary of even one selected person cannot be calculated, nobody in that batch is marked.

**Warning** After a month is marked as paid, Clockyn saves the net amount and a full copy of the calculation. The app has no option to undo it, and later changes to attendance, advances or templates do not change a saved payment. Check View Staff Salary before you tap Mark as paid.

## Previous months and changing figures

Use the month and year picker to open an earlier month. Until a month is marked as paid, the figures follow the current attendance, approved expenses and advances, so they change as the month goes on. After it is marked as paid, the saved figures are shown with a Paid status.

## How a staff member sees their payslip

The staff member opens the Attendance Calendar from Home and taps Payslip. It shows the company name, Earnings, Deductions and the net pay in figures and words. If no payslip is assigned, it says "No payslip assigned yet."

## Common questions

### Why is a net salary lower than the basic salary?

Deductions are taken from earnings: EPF, Professional Tax, Health Insurance, advances given in that month, a late fine and any extra deduction lines. Also, days without punches do not earn daily or hourly pay. Compare each column in View Staff Salary.

### Can I undo a salary I marked as paid by mistake?

The app has no unmark option and a saved payment cannot be overwritten. Please contact support through Support > Live Chat and our team will help.

### Does Mark as paid send money to my staff?

No. It only records in Clockyn that you paid. The bank transfer or cash payment is done by you outside the app.

### Who appears in the Monthly Salary list?

All your active managers and staff. Staff you have deactivated are not in new salary lists.

### Does the salary change during the month?

Yes, until it is marked as paid. It follows the current attendance, approved expenses and advances.

### Can I change the amount of a salary after marking it as paid?

No. The saved amount does not change. If an amount is wrong, contact support before you pay again.

## What can go wrong

- **Pay is zero for a person.** Cause: no payslip, daily or hourly template is assigned. Fix: assign one under Settings > Salary Structure.
- **Hourly pay is lower than expected.** Cause: a forgotten punch-out means the session is not complete yet. Fix: open Staff Details > Day-Wise and correct the punch-out.
- **Overtime or a bonus is missing.** Cause: setup is incomplete. Fix: see [incentives, overtime and bonus](kb:incentives).
- **Payment not processed for a batch.** Cause: one selected person's salary could not be calculated, or more than 500 were selected. Fix: select fewer people or contact support.

## When to contact support

Contact Clockyn support through Support > Live Chat if you marked a salary as paid by mistake, a figure looks wrong after you checked the columns in View Staff Salary, or a batch will not process. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, the staff member's name, the month and year, what you tried and a screenshot of the screen.
