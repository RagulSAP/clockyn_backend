Every new company starts with a free trial. During the trial you can try Clockyn without paying. This guide explains how long it lasts, what is included and what to do before it ends.

## How long the trial lasts

A new company gets a free trial of **1 month**, counted from the day you register. The trial end date is shown under Settings > Your Plan & Validity. On the Home screen the app warns you with a **Free Trial Ending Soon** message before it ends.

## What the trial includes

During the trial all features are available. You can add up to **10 active managers and staff**. The admin account itself is not counted.

## How to see your trial status

This is for the company admin.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Your Plan & Validity**.
3. Look at the badge at the top of the screen. Under it, the card explains what to do next.

The badge can show:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Free Trial | You are still in the starter period that comes with a new company |
| Active | You have a paid plan that is still valid |
| Expired | Your paid plan has passed its Valid Until date |
| No Plan | The free trial is over and you have not bought a plan yet |

The same screen shows **Billing Date**, **Valid Until**, **Staff Limit**, **Used Staff** and **Remaining**. On the **Valid Until** day itself the plan still works.

## Buying a plan during the trial

You do not have to wait for the trial to end. To buy a plan whenever you are ready, tap **Buy a Plan**. The trial buying flow is described in [Buying, renewing and changing plans](kb:buying-renewing-and-changing-plans).

While the trial is running, **Upgrade Plan** and **Renew Plan** are locked. The app shows **Not Available During Free Trial** with the date the trial ends.

## What happens when the trial ends

Your data stays safe. The features that need a plan are switched off until you buy a plan. This includes face punch with the camera, chat, salary structure, staff location tracking and CRM. The app tells you that your free trial and plan have expired and asks you to subscribe. After you buy a plan, everything works again.

## Common questions

### Is a credit card needed to start the trial?
The registration steps do not ask for a card or payment. You only pay when you choose to buy a plan.

### How many staff can I add during the trial?
Up to 10 active managers and staff. The Add Staff screen shows **Staff: X / Y** so you can see how many seats are left.

### Can I upgrade or renew during the trial?
Upgrade Plan and Renew Plan are locked during the trial. You can tap **Buy a Plan** at any time.

### Will I lose my data when the trial ends?
No. Your data stays safe. Features that need a plan switch off until you buy one.

### Is the trial the same on iPhone?
On iPhone and iPad the app only shows your plan status. It does not offer buying, renewing or upgrading. To purchase, use Clockyn on an Android phone or the web version.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face punch, chat, CRM or staff location suddenly stop working | The trial or plan has expired | Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity and buy a plan |
| Add Staff shows "Staff limit reached" | You have used all 10 trial seats | Deactivate someone who left, or buy a plan with a bigger staff count |
| You cannot find Buy a Plan | You are on iPhone or iPad, where buying is not offered | Use an Android phone or the web version |
| Renew Plan is locked | The free trial is still running | Tap Buy a Plan instead |

## When to contact support

For plan questions the trial does not answer, ask in Support > Live Chat. Have these ready:

- Your company name and the admin e-mail address
- The trial end date shown in Settings > Your Plan & Validity
- The exact message the app showed, with a screenshot if possible
