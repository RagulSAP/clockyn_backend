Clockyn has three plans: **Core**, **Prime** and **Elite**. You pay per staff member, per month, and longer durations include free months. This guide explains what each plan includes and how the price on your Payment Summary is worked out. Prices are set by Clockyn and shown in the app. They are not listed here.

## What each plan includes

The features of each plan are listed in **Plan Details**.

| Plan | Features |
| --- | --- |
| Core | Chat, Payslips, Geo Fencing Attendance |
| Prime | Everything in Core, plus GPS Live Location for Field Operations and Location History |
| Elite | Everything in Prime, plus CRM |

During the free trial all features are available. If your plan has expired, features that need a plan are switched off until you renew. See [How the free trial works](kb:free-trial).

## Plan durations and free months

Core, Prime and Elite each come in several durations, for example **1 Month**, **6 Months** and **1 Year**. More durations may be added later. In Plan Details, scroll sideways under a plan to see every duration with its price and what you save.

- The **price per staff per month is the same** on every duration of a plan.
- Longer durations include **free months**. For example, a 6-month plan with 1 free month is charged as 5 months, and a 12-month plan with 2 free months is charged as 10 months.
- You still get the **full time**. A 6-month plan runs for 6 months even though you pay for 5.

Each duration card shows its offer, such as "1 month free", and the saving per staff. The offer on your screen is the one that applies.

## How the price is calculated

The price is: **number of staff × price per staff per month × the months you pay for**.

The Payment Summary then applies these steps in order:

1. **Staff Charges**: staff × price per staff per month × paid months.
2. **Plan Upgrade Credit**: deducted if you have an unused current plan.
3. A **coupon** or a **referral reward** is deducted. You can use one or the other, not both.
4. **Subtotal**: the amount after the steps above.
5. **GST**: 18% is added on top of the subtotal.
6. **Grand Total**: what you pay.

The Payment Summary shows every line before you pay.

### Worked example

You buy a plan for 12 staff, for 6 months, and the duration includes 1 free month. You pay for 5 months, not 6. The staff charge is 12 × the monthly price × 5. Then GST is added. The plan is valid for the full 6 months.

## Staff count

You choose the staff count on the **Set Staff Count** step. The price scales with the number of staff. The staff limit of your plan becomes the number you choose. To add more later, use **Upgrade Plan**. See [Buying, renewing and changing plans](kb:buying-renewing-and-changing-plans).

## Common questions

### Which plan should I choose?
It depends on the features you need. Core covers Chat, Payslips and Geo Fencing Attendance. Prime adds GPS Live Location for Field Operations and Location History. Elite adds CRM. Compare them in Plan Details before you buy.

### Is GST included in the price?
No. 18% GST is added to the amount after discounts. The Payment Summary shows the GST and the Grand Total, and your invoice shows the same details.

### Is the price per month the same for 1 month and 12 months?
Yes, the price per staff per month is the same on every duration. Longer durations give you free months, which lowers what you pay for the whole period.

### Why can I not open CRM or staff location tracking?
These two features are not part of every plan. Staff location tracking needs Prime or Elite, and CRM needs Elite. They are also blocked when your plan has expired. Open Plan Details to compare.

### Can I get a custom plan or a bigger discount?
Plan prices and offers are decided by the Clockyn team. On the Payment Summary there is an option to e-mail the team asking for a better discount coupon. You can also ask in Support > Live Chat.

### Can I buy a plan on an iPhone?
On iPhone and iPad the app only shows your plan status. To purchase, use Clockyn on an Android phone or the web version.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A feature you need is locked | It is not part of your plan, or your plan has expired | Check Settings > Your Plan & Validity and Plan Details |
| The total looks higher than expected | GST is added, or the free months were not on the duration you picked | Read each line on the Payment Summary |
| You cannot find a duration | It is not offered at the moment | Scroll sideways under the plan in Plan Details, or ask in Live Chat |

## When to contact support

For special pricing or any plan question not covered here, please contact Clockyn support in Support > Live Chat. Have these ready:

- Your company name and admin e-mail
- The plan and duration you are looking at
- Your staff count, and a screenshot of the Payment Summary if you have one
