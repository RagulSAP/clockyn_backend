Staff can send work expenses to you from their phone, and you approve or reject each claim. Approved claims are added to the staff member's salary. Separately, you can give a salary advance, which is deducted from salary. This guide covers both.

## How staff submit expenses

On the staff Home screen, a staff member opens the Expenses tile and chooses one of three cards:

- ODO Meter Reading: daily vehicle readings
- Travel Tickets: travel tickets and transport bills
- Other Bills: invoices and miscellaneous expenses

Every submission goes to you for approval. Travel Tickets and Other Bills work the same way (a photo plus an amount). They appear in separate salary columns, Ticket and Other Bills, so staff should choose the right card.

### Record an odometer reading

1. Open Expenses > ODO Meter Reading.
2. Take a photo of the odometer and enter Odometer Reading (KM).
3. Tap Save Start Reading. You will see "Start Recorded!".
4. At the end of the trip, open the same screen, take a photo, enter the stop KM and tap Complete Reading. You will see "Trip Complete!".

Clockyn works out the distance for you. There is one odometer reading per day. A second start reading for the same date is refused with "Start reading already submitted for this date". For several trips, enter the first start KM and the last stop KM of the day. The stop KM must be higher than the start KM.

### Submit a travel ticket or an other bill

1. Open Expenses > Travel Tickets (or Other Bills).
2. Check the date at the top. You can go back to earlier days but not into the future.
3. Take a photo and enter the Amount (more than 0, up to two decimals).
4. Tap Submit Ticket (or Submit Bill).

You will see "Ticket Submitted!" or "Bill Submitted!". If the expense was saved but the photo failed to upload, the app says "Saved, but the photo failed to upload". Do not submit again; tap Retry photo upload. If the camera is blocked the app says "Camera permission is required"; allow the camera for Clockyn in your phone settings.

## Approve or reject claims

1. Open Settings > Expenses & Allowances and choose Odometer, Travel Ticket or Other Bills.
2. Each record shows the employee, date, amount and photo. Open the photos first.
3. Tap Approve or Reject and confirm.

**Warning** Approve and reject are final. Clockyn warns "This cannot be changed later." The buttons show only while a claim is pending.

Approved odometer readings, travel tickets and other bills are added to the staff member's earnings for the month of the reading or expense date. They show in the Odometer, Ticket and Other Bills columns of View Staff Salary. Pending and rejected claims are not added.

## Odometer allowance and the rate per km

The allowance is distance travelled multiplied by the rate per km. Until you set a rate, the app shows ₹3 per km. Example: a trip of 42.5 km at ₹3 gives ₹127.50.

To change the rate:

1. Open Settings > Expenses & Allowances > Odometer.
2. Tap edit on the rate card (Edit Allowance Rate).
3. Enter Rate per KM (₹) and tap Save Rate. The app saves whole rupees only.

The new rate applies to readings still pending. Approved and rejected readings keep the rate they had. If a reading does not match its photos, open the Odometer Photos (Start Reading and Stop Reading) and tap Reject while it is pending.

## Give a salary advance

Only the admin can give an advance, to a manager or staff member of your organization (not to the admin account).

1. Open the staff list from Home and tap the staff member to see Staff Details.
2. Open the Advance tab and choose Add Advance.
3. Pick the Date (it cannot be in the future) and enter the Advance Amount (₹), more than 0.
4. Tap Save Advance.

The record appears under View Advance. The total of advances dated within a month is deducted from that month's salary, shown in the Advance (₹) column and as the Advance deduction on the payslip. For example, an advance of ₹5,000 dated 12 March is deducted in the March salary, so choose the date carefully.

Clockyn has no option to edit or delete a saved advance. Staff can see their advances by opening the Attendance Calendar from Home and tapping Advance Salary.

## Advance or expense claim?

An advance is money you give in advance and it is deducted from salary. An expense claim is money the employee spent for work, which you approve and which is added to salary. An advance is a deduction; an expense is an earning.

## Common questions

### Can a staff member record more than one odometer trip in a day?

No. There is one reading per day. Enter the first start KM and the final stop KM.

### Can I change my decision after approving an expense?

No. Approve and reject are final.

### Why do I see "Stop reading must be greater than start reading"?

The stop KM must be higher than the start KM for that day. Equal readings are not accepted. Check the figure and that start and stop are not swapped.

### Who can give advances?

Only the admin. Other users see "Only admin (role_id = 1) can assign advances".

### Where do approved expenses show in the salary?

In the Odometer, Ticket and Other Bills columns of Settings > View & Download Reports > Salary Reports > View Staff Salary.

### Can I edit an advance I entered by mistake?

The app does not show an edit or delete option. Contact support before that month's salary is marked as paid.

## What can go wrong

- **"Please upload image and enter KM value".** Cause: the photo or the reading is missing. Fix: tap Take Photo, type the kilometres and save again.
- **"Advance date cannot be in the future".** Cause: the date is after today. Fix: pick today or an earlier date.
- **"Amount must be greater than 0".** Cause: the amount is zero or not a number. Fix: enter an amount above zero.
- **An expense does not appear in salary.** Cause: it is still pending or was rejected. Fix: approve it under Expenses & Allowances.

## When to contact support

If an odometer reading was entered wrongly and cannot be corrected, or an advance was entered by mistake, contact Clockyn support through Support > Live Chat before the month's salary is marked as paid. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, the staff member's name, the date and amount, what you tried, and a screenshot.
