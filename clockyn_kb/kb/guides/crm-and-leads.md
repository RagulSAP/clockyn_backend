CRM helps you collect client leads and hand them to your staff. As admin you add leads, assign them and follow their progress. Staff and managers see their own tasks and update the status as they work. CRM is a plan feature: it is available on the Elite plan, and the Core and Prime plans do not include it. See "CRM Unavailable" below if you cannot open it.

## What the admin does in CRM

The admin works in four places:

- Enter Leads: add a new lead.
- View / Assign Leads: see leads by date, edit, delete, assign and reassign them.
- Work Status Overview: see how the work is going.
- Carry Forward Requests: approve or reject requests from staff to move a lead to another date.

## Add a lead

1. Open CRM > Enter Leads.
2. Fill Name, Mobile, Address and Category. Remarks and the customer required date are optional.
3. Tap Save Lead.

If a field is missing you will see "Please fill all required fields". The mobile number must be exactly 10 digits. The field accepts digits only and stops at 10, so leave out country codes, spaces and dashes.

### Create a new category

1. While entering or editing a lead, tap the Category field to open Select Category.
2. Tap Add and type the name under Category name.
3. Tap Add again to save.

Category names are not case sensitive, so "Category already exists" appears if the name is in use. You can search the list and delete a category you no longer need.

## Assign a lead to a staff member

1. Open CRM > View / Assign Leads.
2. Pick the day with the date chips (All, Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow or Pick Date).
3. Tap Assign on the lead.
4. Filter by Branch or Department if needed, then tap Assign next to the employee.

A success pop-up confirms it, and the employee sees the lead in My Leads. A lead is handled by one employee at a time. Assigning the same lead to the same person again returns "Lead is already assigned". To change the person, tap Reassign on the lead; afterwards only the new employee is assigned.

## Edit or delete a lead

In CRM > View / Assign Leads each lead card has Edit, Delete and Assign buttons. Edit opens the lead form, and you tap Update Lead to save. Delete asks "Are you sure you want to delete this lead?" and removes it for good.

Only leads that are not completed can be changed. A lead marked Completed is locked: the card shows a Completed badge, the buttons are disabled and tapping it says "Completed leads cannot be assigned". If a client needs a new job, add a fresh lead.

## Lead status and the Work Status Overview

Status shows the progress of the employee's work on the lead:

- Pending: assigned, not started
- Working: the employee is on it
- Completed: finished (the lead is then locked for you)
- Carry Forward: the employee has asked to move it to another date and waits for your decision

Work Status Overview shows tiles for Total, Unassigned, Pending, Working, Completed and Carry Fwd leads for the selected day, followed by a Lead-wise Status list. Tap a tile to filter the list, for example Unassigned shows the leads that still need a staff member. The same date chips filter the overview.

## Carry forward requests

When an employee cannot finish a lead today, they choose Carry Forward in the status sheet, write a Reason, pick the Forward to date (tomorrow by default) and tap Send Request. They see "Request Sent!".

To decide as admin:

1. Open CRM and go to Carry Forward Requests. A red badge shows how many are pending.
2. Read the Employee, Reason, Requested for date and Submitted on.
3. Tap Approve (confirm "Yes, Approve") or Reject.

Approval moves the lead to the requested date. Rejecting puts the lead back to the status it had before. A request can be actioned only once.

## What staff and managers see

Staff and managers open CRM > My Leads. It lists the leads assigned to them that are due today or have no date. They filter with All, Pending, Working or Completed, tap a lead to open the status sheet and choose a new status. Opening My Leads clears the new-lead badge on the CRM tab.

Staff and managers can also add leads: tap Add Lead on My Leads and fill the Add New Lead sheet (name, a 10-digit mobile, address and category, with optional remarks). They see "Lead saved and sent to your admin for assignment", and the lead appears as Unassigned for you in View / Assign Leads.

## Common questions

### Why does CRM say "CRM Unavailable"?

Your plan does not include CRM, or the plan has expired. The admin sees a Renew Plan button (View Plan Status on iPhone) that opens Your Plan & Validity. Managers and staff only see the message and should ask their admin. Check Settings > Your Plan & Validity.

### My employee cannot see an assigned lead. Why?

My Leads shows leads assigned to the employee that are due today or have no due date. A lead dated for another day appears on that day. Ask them to pull down to refresh, and check the lead's date and assignee in View / Assign Leads.

### Can a lead be assigned to two people?

No. One employee handles a lead at a time. Use Reassign to change the person.

### Why are Edit, Delete and Assign disabled?

The lead is Completed and therefore locked. Add a fresh lead for new work.

### Why can my employee not send a carry forward request?

Clockyn refuses it when the reason is empty ("Description is required"), the date is in the past ("forward_date cannot be in the past"), a request for the same lead is already waiting ("A pending carry forward request already exists"), or the lead is not assigned to that employee.

### How can I search leads by date?

Use the date chips in View / Assign Leads. If nothing matches you see "No leads found for this date".

## What can go wrong

- **"Mobile number must be exactly 10 digits".** Cause: the number has more or fewer than 10 digits. Fix: enter 10 digits only.
- **"Category already exists".** Cause: same name in another case. Fix: select the existing category.
- **A carry forward request cannot be actioned twice.** Cause: it is already approved or rejected. Fix: refresh the list.
- **The CRM tab shows a Renew Plan button.** Cause: the plan lacks CRM or has expired. Fix: open Your Plan & Validity.

## When to contact support

If CRM stays unavailable after you renewed or changed your plan, or a lead problem persists after you tried the steps here, contact Clockyn support through Support > Live Chat. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, your current plan, what you tried and a screenshot of the message.
