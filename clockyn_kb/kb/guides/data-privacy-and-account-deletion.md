This guide explains how to ask Clockyn to delete your account, what happens right after you ask, and how deactivating a staff member differs from deleting them. Account deletion cannot be undone, and questions about exactly which data is kept are decided by the Clockyn team, so this topic is handled by a person at Clockyn support. Nothing here is a legal promise; if you need a written confirmation of what is stored or removed, ask support.

## Where to read the privacy policy

As admin, open Settings > Beyond > More Options > Privacy Policy. The page opens inside the app. Tap I Understand, Go Back to return.

## Request deletion of an admin account

1. Open Settings > Beyond > More Options > Privacy Policy.
2. Tap Request Account Deletion.
3. Read the message and confirm with Submit Request.

The confirmation says your deletion request will be sent to the admin, and once reviewed, all your data will be permanently deleted. The request is reviewed by the Clockyn team. Right after you submit, the app marks your session inactive and signs you out. Nothing is removed until the request has been reviewed.

If you send the request again while it is still waiting, you see "Deletion request already submitted. Awaiting admin review." The app has no cancel button for a request. If you changed your mind, message Clockyn support in Support > Live Chat as soon as possible.

## What deletion removes, and what it does not

When Clockyn processes a deletion for a company account, the company, its staff and their records are removed or made anonymous so they can no longer be tied to a person. This covers the staff and manager accounts and their personal details, attendance records, face data, the contact details of clients in leads, and the company details. For exactly which records are deleted and which are only made anonymous, please ask support.

Clockyn's deletion steps do not remove the billing records, which are the orders, payments, invoices and subscriptions for your plan. If you need to know how long billing records are kept, or want to know exactly what is stored about you, please contact Clockyn support.

After deletion, nobody can sign in with the deleted account. The app says "Your account has been deleted." To start again, an admin registers again or adds staff again.

## If you are a manager or staff member

Managers and staff have their own option. Open Settings and then More Options, and tap Delete My Account. The app asks "Delete Account?" and says it will permanently delete your account and all associated data and that this cannot be undone. This applies to your own account, not to the company. The admin account, the company and the other staff are not removed this way.

## Deactivate or permanently delete a staff member

Deactivate is reversible. The person cannot punch in or sign in, and you can switch them back on.

1. Open the person's Staff Details and switch Active off, or use the switch in the staff list.
2. Confirm Deactivate.

Permanent delete removes the person and all their records (attendance, salary, requests, chat and photos) and cannot be undone. Only the organization admin can do this, and the admin account itself can never be deleted this way.

1. Open the person's Staff Details.
2. Tap the trash icon (Delete staff permanently).
3. Type the person's name to confirm.
4. Tap Delete.

**Warning** Permanent delete removes the records for good. Use deactivate for someone who may return. A manager who still has staff reporting to them cannot be deleted until you reassign those staff.

## What happens when you log out

Nothing is deleted. Logging out only signs this device out and forgets its trusted-device status, so the next sign-in needs an OTP. Your company, staff and attendance data stay as they are.

## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

Open Settings > Beyond > More Options > Privacy Policy, tap Request Account Deletion and confirm with Submit Request. The Clockyn team reviews the request.

### Can I cancel an account deletion request?

The app has no cancel button. Message Clockyn support in Support > Live Chat as soon as possible.

### Is my data deleted when I log out?

No. Logging out only signs the device out.

### What is the difference between deactivating and deleting a staff member?

Deactivate is reversible; permanent delete removes the person and their records for good.

### Will my payment and invoice records be deleted?

Clockyn's deletion steps do not remove billing records. For details on how long they are kept, contact Clockyn support.

### Can a manager delete a staff member?

No. Only the organization admin sees the permanent delete option.

## What can go wrong

- **"Deletion request already submitted. Awaiting admin review."** Cause: you already sent a request. Fix: wait for the review, or message support.
- **"Your account has been deleted" at sign-in.** Cause: the account was removed after a permanent delete or an approved deletion request. Fix: register again, or contact support for details.
- **A manager cannot be deleted.** Cause: staff still report to them. Fix: reassign those staff first.

## When to contact support

Contact Clockyn support through Support > Live Chat for any deletion question, to cancel a request, or to ask what data is kept. Because this involves your account, please have ready your company name, the admin e-mail and mobile number you registered with, and what you want to happen. Do not send passwords or OTPs in the chat.
