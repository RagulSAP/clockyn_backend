The Chat tab lets you talk to your staff and send announcements. You can message one person, make a group, or announce something to everyone or to a branch or department. Chat works on every Clockyn plan while your plan is active, and is unavailable when the plan has expired. For talking to Clockyn support, see [contacting support and Live Chat](kb:contacting-support-and-live-chat); that is a different chat.

## Chat with one person

1. Open the Chat tab and tap Individual Chat.
2. In the search box type a name, branch or department.
3. Tap the person to open the conversation and type your message.

People you already talked to stay in the list. Staff can message colleagues of the same organization in the same way.

### Photos and documents

In the message box tap the camera icon to take a photo, or the attach icon to choose Photo library or Document. While a file uploads, the camera and attach buttons are disabled. The default file size limit is 25 MB. If a file fails to send you can retry or discard it.

### What the tick marks mean

- Clock: the message is still sending
- Red alert icon: it failed to send, and you can retry
- One tick: sent
- Two ticks: read by the other person

### Delete a chat or see contact details

To delete, open the conversation, tap the menu in the header, choose Delete Chat and confirm. Clockyn says "This cannot be undone", and the whole conversation is removed for both people. Use Search in the same menu to look for something first. To see a person's details, tap their name or choose View Contact: it shows Emp ID, Mobile, Email, Department, Branch and Role.

## Group chats

Only the admin can create groups.

1. Open Chat > Group Chat.
2. Tap Create Group.
3. Type the Group name (up to 50 characters) and select the members. At least one member is required.
4. Tap Create. You will see "Group created!".

To add people later, open the group, tap the header menu, choose Add Staff, select the people and tap Add. Any member of the group can add staff. Only active staff who are not in the group are listed. Use View Members to see everyone, with the group admin marked as Admin.

Clockyn currently has no Remove Member or Leave Group option. If someone must be removed, contact support. The admin can pick a Chat Theme (Default, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Lavender, Rose, Dark or Teal); it is saved on that device only.

## Send an announcement

Only the admin can send announcements; staff can read them.

### To all staff

1. Open Chat > Announcement and stay on the Compose tab.
2. Enter the Title (up to 100 characters) and Message (up to 1000).
3. Under Send To keep All Staff.
4. Tap Send to All Staff.

You see "All staff have been notified". Every active manager and staff member gets a notification. Staff you have deactivated, and the admin account that sends it, do not receive it.

### To selected people

1. Open Chat > Announcement and write the Title and Message.
2. Under Send To choose Select Staff.
3. Search and tick the people in Select Staff, then tap Done.
4. Tap the send button, for example Send to 5 Staff.

### To a branch or department

1. Open Chat > Branch & Dept.
2. Choose Branch or Department and pick the one you want under Select Branch or Select Department.
3. Write the Title and Message.
4. Tap the send button, for example Send to Mumbai.

Only active managers and staff of that branch or department receive it.

### History, edit and delete

Switch from Compose to History to see past announcements, most recent first, with the count (for example History (12)). Search by title; in Branch & Dept you can also search by branch or department name. You can delete an announcement but not edit it. Tap Delete in History and confirm "Delete Announcement?". It is removed for good and staff no longer see it. To correct a mistake, delete it and send a new one.

## What staff see

Staff get a notification. In the Chat tab they open Broadcast (announcements from the admin) or Targeted Announcement (for their branch or department), and can search by text.

## Common questions

### Why does the Chat tab say "Feature Unavailable" or "Chat Unavailable"?

Your plan has expired or is not active. The admin sees "Feature Unavailable" with a Renew Plan button (View Plan Status on iPhone). Managers and staff see "Chat Unavailable" and should ask the admin. Check Settings > Your Plan & Validity.

### Why can I not send my announcement?

The send button stays disabled until everything is filled. You may see "Title is required", "Message is required", "Select at least 1 staff member" (for Select Staff), or "Select a branch" or "Select a department" (for Branch & Dept).

### Can I edit an announcement after sending?

No. Delete it from History and send a new one.

### Who receives an announcement sent to All Staff?

All active managers and staff of your organization.

### Can I remove someone from a group?

Not in the app today. Contact support.

### Can staff create groups?

No. Only the admin creates groups. Staff see the groups they were added to.

## What can go wrong

- **A file will not send.** Cause: the file is over the size limit or the connection dropped. Fix: retry, or send a smaller file.
- **A staff member did not get an announcement.** Cause: they are deactivated or not in the chosen branch or department. Fix: check the recipients in History.
- **Messages show a red alert icon.** Cause: sending failed. Fix: tap to retry and check the internet connection.

## When to contact support

If Chat stays unavailable after your plan is active, or you need someone removed from a group, contact Clockyn support through Support > Live Chat. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, the group or person involved, what you tried and a screenshot.
