Refer & Earn lets you invite other companies to Clockyn. You share your own referral code. When a new company enters it while registering, both companies get a discount on a plan. Refer & Earn is available to the company admin. The discount percent is set by Clockyn and is shown on the Refer & Earn screen. It can change over time, so this guide does not quote a number.

## How it works

- The company you referred gets the discount on its **first plan** after the free trial.
- You get the same discount on your **next plan or renewal**, once they have paid for their first plan.
- The reward works on **Prime and Elite** plans only.

A reward keeps the percent it had when it was issued. If Clockyn changes the percent later, only new referrals get the new percent. Rewards from a friend's code do not expire.

## Find and share your referral code

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Refer & Earn**. It is the row right after Your Plan & Validity.
2. Your 6-character code is shown in large letters at the top. Tap the code or **Copy code** to copy it.
3. Tap **Share** to use your phone's share sheet, **WhatsApp** to send it in WhatsApp, or **SMS** to send it as a text message. **Copy message** copies a ready-made invitation that includes your code.

Every company has one code, and it does not change.

## How the company you refer uses your code

The new company types your code in the optional **Referral code** box on the register screen, when it creates its Clockyn account. When the code is right, the screen shows **Referred by** followed by your company name and the discount it will get.

- The code can only be entered while registering. It cannot be added to an account that already exists.
- A company cannot use its own code.
- A company can be referred only once.

## Where you see who you referred

Open Settings > Refer & Earn. Under **Organisations you referred**, each company shows its Joined date, a **Signed up** or **Paid** label, and the status of your reward:

- **Pending**: unlocks when they pay for their first plan.
- **Available**: you can use it on your next plan or renewal.
- **Used**: shows the plan and the amount saved.
- **Void**: the reward was withdrawn.

Your rewards show the Available, Pending and Used counts.

## Use your reward when you pay

1. Open Settings > Your Plan & Validity and choose your plan (or renew), then continue to the Payment Summary.
2. Under the coupon box you see a **Referral reward** card with its discount percent and the number of rewards you have available.
3. Tap **Apply**. The card turns green and the price breakdown shows the Referral reward with its percent and your new Grand Total.
4. Tap Pay as usual. To change your mind before paying, tap **Remove**.

The reward is used up only when the payment goes through. The discount is taken off the staff charge before GST, after any plan credit. On iPhone the app only shows your plan status, so do this on an Android phone or the web version.

## Which purchases the reward works on

- It works on a first plan after the free trial, or on a renewal.
- It works on **Prime and Elite** only. It does not work on Core.
- It does not work when you add staff with Upgrade Plan.
- A purchase can use a coupon or a referral reward, not both.

If you pick a plan it does not cover, the Apply button is greyed out and the card says why.

## Common questions

### I referred more than one company. Can I use the reward more than once?
Yes. Every company you refer gives you one reward, and you can use one reward per purchase. Each reward is available once the company you referred has paid for its first plan.

### Can I use a referral reward and a coupon together?
No. A purchase can use either a coupon or a referral reward, not both. If you apply one while the other is applied, the first one is removed and the app tells you.

### Why can I not apply my reward?
Check these: the reward is still Pending because the company has not paid yet, you picked the Core plan, you are adding staff with Upgrade Plan, or you already applied a coupon. If none fits, ask in Support > Live Chat.

### Can I add a referral code after I registered?
No. A code can only be entered on the register screen when the company is created.

### Where can I see how much I saved?
Under Settings > Refer & Earn. A used reward shows the plan and the amount saved.

### What is the difference between a friend's code and a partner promo code?
A friend's code comes from another Clockyn company. A partner promo code comes from a Clockyn partner such as a YouTube or Instagram channel. See [Partner promo codes](kb:partner-promo-codes).

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apply is greyed out on the Payment Summary | The plan or purchase is not covered, or a coupon is applied | Read the reason on the card, pick Prime or Elite, or remove the coupon |
| Your reward still shows Pending | The company you referred has not paid for its first plan | Wait. It becomes Available after that payment |
| The code is refused at registration | It is mistyped, switched off, or you are past registration | Check the 6 characters carefully |

## When to contact support

If your reward is missing or will not apply, ask in Support > Live Chat. Have these ready:

- Your company name and admin e-mail
- The name of the company you referred, and when it joined
- A screenshot of Settings > Refer & Earn and of the Payment Summary
