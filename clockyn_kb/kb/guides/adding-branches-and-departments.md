A **branch** is one office or site of your company. A **department** is a group of people inside the company, such as Sales or HR. Staff belong to a branch and a department, and branches have a GPS location so that attendance can be checked against where people are.

## Add a branch

This is done by the company admin, or a manager who has been given Branch Management permission.

1. On Home, open **Manage > Branches** and tap **Add Branch**. You can also use **Add Branch** on the Branches card.
2. Enter **Branch Name** and **Branch Address**.
3. Tap the **GPS Location** box to find the branch on the map, then tap **Save Location**.
4. Choose the **Attendance Radius**.
5. Tap **Add Branch**.

Name, address and GPS location are all required.

## What the attendance radius means

The attendance radius is how close a staff member must be to the branch to punch in. You can choose **100, 250, 500, 750 or 1000 metres**. The default is 100 m. If staff are outside the radius, the app says how far away they are, for example "You are 250m away from your branch. Must be within 100m to punch." Field staff are not limited by the radius. See [Adding and managing staff](kb:adding-and-managing-staff) for how to mark someone as Field Staff.

## Change a branch's GPS location

1. Open Home > Manage > Branches and tap the pencil on the branch.
2. Tap the **GPS Location** box.
3. Stand at the branch and let the app find your position, or move the pin on the map.
4. Tap **Save Location** (it says **Update Location** if one is already set).
5. Finish with **Update Branch**.

## Add a department

1. On Home open **Manage > Departments** and tap **Add Dept**.
2. Type the **Department Name**, for example Sales, HR or Engineering.
3. Tap **Save Department**.

Department names must be unique inside your company. You can also list departments while adding a company.

## Delete a branch

Deleting cannot be undone.

1. Open Home > Manage > Branches.
2. Tap the trash icon on the branch.
3. Confirm **Delete Branch**.

If staff are still assigned to the branch, the app first asks you to move them. It shows "N staff in this branch must be reassigned before deletion". Select the staff (or Select All), pick another branch, and tap **Reassign All & Delete Branch**. You can also tap **Reassign** to move only some of them first.

## Delete a department

When you delete a department, the people in it are left without a department. Edit each of them and choose a new **Department Name**. The Delete Department prompt cannot be undone, so read it carefully.

## Common questions

### Why does it say "Branch name already exists"?
Each branch in a company needs its own name. Pick a different name, for example add the city or area. Check the branch list first, because the name may belong to an existing branch.

### Why does it say "Department name already exists"?
Department names must be unique in your company. Choose another name or edit the existing department. The same message appears if you rename a department to a name another one already has.

### Can I delete a branch that still has staff?
Not directly. Move the staff to another branch first, using the reassign step the app shows.

### Which radius should I choose?
The radius is your choice and depends on the site. A smaller radius is stricter. Choose one of 100, 250, 500, 750 or 1000 metres. The default is 100 m.

### Can I have more than one company with different branches?
Yes. Each company has its own branches, departments, staff and shifts. See [Getting started: register your company](kb:getting-started-register-company).

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add Branch will not save | Branch name, address or GPS location is missing | Fill all three. The GPS location must be saved with Save Location |
| Staff cannot punch in at the branch | They are outside the attendance radius, or the GPS pin is in the wrong place | Check the branch location and radius, or turn on Field Staff for people who work outside |
| "Branch name already exists" | Another branch uses that name | Choose a different name |
| Staff vanish from a department list | The department was deleted | Edit each person and choose a department |

## When to contact support

If a branch cannot be added or deleted and the steps above do not help, ask in Support > Live Chat. Have these ready:

- Your company name and admin e-mail
- The branch name and what you tried
- The exact message the app showed, with a screenshot if you can
