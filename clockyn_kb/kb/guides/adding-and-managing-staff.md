Staff are the people who punch in with Clockyn. As the admin you add them, assign them to a branch and department, and can deactivate them when they leave. This guide covers the whole life of a staff member in the app.

## Add a staff member

1. On Home open **Manage > Staff** and tap **Add Staff**.
2. Fill in **Full Name**, **Employee ID**, **Phone Number**, **Email Address**, **Branch Name**, **Department Name** and **Gender**.
3. Optionally pick a **Shift** and switch on **Field Staff**.
4. Add a photo or **More details** if you wish.
5. Tap **Add Staff**.

Required: Full Name, Employee ID, Phone Number, Branch Name, Department Name and Gender. Email Address is also required when your Clockyn signs in by e-mail, because staff need it to sign in. Everything under More details (date of birth, blood group, emergency contact, address and bank details) is optional.

## How staff sign in and start

1. Add them with a correct e-mail address. Staff you have not added cannot sign in.
2. Ask them to install the app and choose **Employee Access**.
3. They enter their e-mail, tap **Get OTP** and enter the code.
4. They allow Camera and Location when asked.
5. On the first punch they register their face with 4 photos.

## Employee ID

The app suggests the next free Employee ID when you open Add Staff, and you can type your own. Each ID must be unique in your company, otherwise you see "Employee ID already exists".

## How many staff you can add

While you are on the free trial or have no active plan, you can have up to 10 active managers and staff. With a paid plan the limit is the one in your plan. The Add Staff screen shows **Staff: X / Y** with the slots remaining. Only active managers and staff are counted, and the admin account is not.

If you see "Staff limit reached", tap **Upgrade** on the red banner (on iOS it says **View Status**) or open Settings > Your Plan & Validity. You can also free a seat by deactivating someone who has left. See [Buying, renewing and changing plans](kb:buying-renewing-and-changing-plans).

## Find and edit a staff member

1. Open Home > Manage > View Staff.
2. Type in the search box or use the chips **All**, **Staff**, **Manager** or **Inactive**.
3. Tap a person to open their **Staff Details**.
4. Tap **Edit** (on the card) or Profile > Edit Staff, change the fields and tap **Update Staff**.

To change a branch or department, change **Branch Name** or **Department Name** in Edit and tap Update Staff. If you leave with unsaved changes, the app asks whether to Save, Exit or Stay.

## Deactivate or reactivate

Deactivate when someone leaves or is away. It can be reversed.

1. Open the person's Staff Details and switch **Active** off, or use the switch in the staff list.
2. Confirm **Deactivate**.

They can no longer punch in or sign in. To bring them back, open Home > Manage > View Staff, choose the **Inactive** chip, find the person and switch **Active** on. The staff limit is checked again, so you need a free seat.

## Delete permanently

Only the organization admin can do this, and it cannot be undone. All their records (attendance, salary, requests, chat and photos) are removed for good.

1. Open the person's Staff Details.
2. Tap the trash icon (Delete staff permanently).
3. Type the person's name to confirm.
4. Tap **Delete**.

> **Warning** Use Deactivate for anyone who may return. Permanent delete removes all of their records.

A manager who still has staff reporting to them cannot be deleted. See [Managers and permissions](kb:managers-and-permissions).

## Field staff and shifts

**Field Staff** can punch from anywhere, so the branch attendance radius does not apply to them. Switch it on in Add Staff or Edit Staff. To give someone a shift, use the **Shift** field in Add or Edit Staff, or tap **Assign** on their Staff Details.

## Common questions

### Why does it say "Employee ID, mobile number, or email already exists"?
Another person already uses the same Employee ID, phone number or e-mail. Search the staff list and change the value you typed. Each staff e-mail must be unique, because it is how they sign in.

### Do inactive staff count towards my limit?
No. Only active managers and staff are counted. Reactivating someone takes a seat again.

### A staff member sees "There is no user with this email address". Why?
Employee sign-in only works for people already added by an admin. Check the e-mail in Staff > open the person > Edit. If it is wrong, correct it. If the person was never added, use Add Staff first.

### What is the difference between deactivating and deleting?
Deactivate is reversible. Permanent delete removes the person and all their records and cannot be undone.

### Can a staff member register their face again?
The app has no Re-register button. If the face is not recognised, follow the photo tips: good light, no mask, steady phone at eye level. If it still does not work, contact support.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Staff limit reached" | All seats of your plan are used | Upgrade the staff count, or deactivate someone who left |
| "Your account is inactive" for a staff member | They were deactivated | Switch them Active in the Inactive chip, if you have a free seat |
| Face registration keeps failing | Poor light, mask, or phone held too close | Face a light, remove the mask, hold the phone at eye level and keep still |
| Staff cannot sign in | They were not added, or the e-mail does not match | Check the e-mail in Edit Staff |

## When to contact support

Ask in Support > Live Chat if the steps above do not solve it. Have these ready:

- Your company name and admin e-mail
- The staff member's name and the e-mail in their record
- The exact message shown, with a screenshot if you can
