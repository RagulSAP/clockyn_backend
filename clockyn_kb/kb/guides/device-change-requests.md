A device change request is how a manager or staff member asks the admin for permission to use Clockyn on a new phone. You need it when someone buys a new phone, loses theirs or breaks it, and the account is tied to one phone. This guide explains both sides: the employee who asks and the admin who approves.

## When you need it

If an employee tries to log in on a new phone and sees the message **You are logging in from a different device. Tap "Request Access" to submit a device change request to your admin.**, the account is tied to the old phone. They must ask the admin for access.

Admins are not tied to one phone. Only managers and staff can be asked to request access. If your staff are not asked to request access, logging in on a new phone with the one-time code simply moves the session to the new phone. Whether the one-phone rule applies to your account is controlled by Clockyn. Please contact Clockyn support if you are not sure.

## Request access (staff and managers)

The easy way, from the login screen:

1. On the new phone start the staff login with your mobile number and the one-time code.
2. When the message about a different device appears, tap **Request Access**.
3. Enter the reason, for example "Lost phone, damaged device, new phone".
4. Tap **Submit Request**. You see **Submitted!** and "Device change request submitted successfully".

Until the admin decides, the login screen says: "Your device change request is pending admin approval. You will be able to login once approved."

If you are still logged in on the old phone you can also open **Requests**, choose **Device Change Request**, fill **Reason for Device Change**, and tap **Submit Request**.

## Limits

- Only **one** device change request can be pending at a time. A second attempt returns the one that is already pending: "A device change request is already pending".
- At most **3** requests can be sent in 24 hours. After that the app says "Too many device change requests. Please try again later."
- A reason is required.

## Approve or reject (admin)

Who can decide: the admin, and managers who have the **Requests Management** permission and the employee in their assigned branches and departments.

1. On **Home**, tap **Requests** in the Quick Actions.
2. Open **Device Change Requests**. The badge counts the ones waiting.
3. Check the employee, the reason and the device details on the card.
4. Tap **Approve** or **Reject**.

The employee is notified of the decision. You can change a decision later with **Change to Approve** or **Change to Reject**.

## What approval does

On the first approval Clockyn signs the old phone out and clears its alert registration, so the old phone stops receiving notifications. The employee can then log in on the new phone with the one-time code. Approving the same request again does not repeat this. If the request is rejected, the employee cannot log in on the new phone.

## Do not confuse it with Assign Devices

**Settings > Attendance & Devices > Assign Devices** lists attendance hardware such as fingerprint, iris, face and RF tag devices, and each shows that it is coming soon. It is not used for staff phones.

## Common questions

### Does the employee need to register the face again on the new phone?
No. The face is checked by Clockyn, so the employee can punch from the phone they log in with.

### Can the admin log in on a new phone without a request?
Yes. Admins can use more than one phone.

### How long does approval take?
It happens as soon as an admin or an allowed manager taps **Approve**. Employees see the decision as a notification.

### The staff member cannot find Request Access
The button appears after the one-time code step, when the app detects a different device. If it does not appear, the account may not be tied to one phone, and a normal login on the new phone should work.

### Can a manager approve requests?
Yes, with the **Requests Management** permission.

### What if the old phone is lost?
Approve the request. The old phone is signed out when you approve.

## What can go wrong

- **"A device change request is already pending":** wait for the admin to decide.
- **"Too many device change requests":** three requests were sent within 24 hours. Try again later.
- **No notification after approval:** the new phone has not allowed notifications yet, see [App permissions and notifications](kb:notifications-and-permissions). The employee can still log in.
- **Still blocked after approval:** the request may be rejected, or the employee is using a third phone. Submit a new request from the phone in use.

## When to contact support

Contact Clockyn support through **Support > Live Chat** if an approved employee still cannot log in, or you are the admin and cannot log in on a new phone. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, the employee name, the mobile number used for login, the exact message on screen and a screenshot.
