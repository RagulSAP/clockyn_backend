Registering creates your Clockyn company account and makes you its administrator (admin). It takes a few minutes. Once you are registered, your free trial starts the same day.

## What you need before you start

In the Clockyn app, the registration form asks for these details. The first six are required.

- **Company Name**
- **Company Address**
- **State**
- **District**
- **Pincode** (6 digits)
- **At least one Branch** (a branch is one office or site)
- **Mobile Number** and **Email ID**, which you confirm with a one-time code

These are optional and can be added later: **GST Number** (15 characters), **Departments**, and, under Add More Details, **PAN Number**, **Company Website**, **CIN Number**, **TAN Number**, **MSME Number** and **Business Category**. If a tax number looks wrong, the app only shows a hint. It does not stop you from registering.

## How to register your company

This is done by the person who will own the account.

1. Open the Clockyn app and choose **Admin Portal**.
2. Enter your e-mail address and tap **Get OTP**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your inbox. If this e-mail has no account yet, the app takes you straight to **Register Company**.
4. Fill in Company Name, Company Address, State, District and Pincode.
5. Add at least one Branch. You can add departments too.
6. Confirm your contact details with the one-time codes the screen asks for.
7. If someone gave you a referral or promo code, type it in the optional **Referral code** box. See [Partner promo codes](kb:partner-promo-codes).
8. Tap **Register Company**.

> **Tip** Registration is for company owners. Managers and staff do not register. The admin adds them, and they use **Employee Access** to sign in.

## Referral codes at registration

The **Referral code (optional)** box is on the register screen only. If the code is valid, the screen shows who referred you and the discount. You cannot add a code after your company has been created, and a company can use only one code.

## What to set up first

A good order for a new company:

1. Open Settings > Company Settings > View Company and add your logo.
2. Add your branches with GPS location and radius (Home > Manage > Branches). See [Adding branches and departments](kb:adding-branches-and-departments).
3. Add departments if you need more than the default ones.
4. Create shifts (Settings > Attendance & Devices > Shift Setting).
5. Add your staff (Home > Manage > Staff > Add Staff). See [Adding and managing staff](kb:adding-and-managing-staff).
6. Set the Attendance Rule and holidays.

Staff can then sign in and punch with their face.

## Common questions

### Do I need a GST number to register?
No. The GST Number is optional. If you enter one it must be 15 characters in the usual GST format. You can add or change it later in Settings > Company Settings > View Company > Edit Company.

### I already have an account but the app shows Register Company. Why?
The e-mail you typed has no admin account yet, so the app starts registration. Check the e-mail for typing mistakes. Tap **Change email** to fix it.

### What does "Email already registered" mean?
That e-mail already has an admin account. Go back to the first screen, choose **Admin Portal** and sign in with it instead of registering. If you do not remember registering, use a different e-mail or contact support.

### Can I use Clockyn on a computer?
Yes. Clockyn runs on Android, iOS and in a web browser. You sign in the same way on every device, with your e-mail and a one-time code.

### Can I run more than one company?
Yes. After registering you can add more companies under Settings > Company Settings > Add Company and switch between them from Home. Each company has its own branches, departments and staff.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You do not receive the one-time code | The e-mail is mistyped or the code went to spam | Check the address with Change email, look in spam, then tap Get OTP again |
| Pincode is refused | It is not 6 digits | Enter a 6-digit pincode |
| Register Company will not continue | A required field is empty, or no branch has been added | Fill in the highlighted fields and add at least one branch |
| "This is an employee account" | The e-mail belongs to a manager or staff member | Go back and choose Employee Access |
| The referral code is not accepted | The code is mistyped, switched off, or you are past registration | Check it carefully. See [Partner promo codes](kb:partner-promo-codes) |

## When to contact support

Contact Clockyn support in the app under Support > Live Chat if you cannot finish registering. Have these ready:

- The company name you tried to register
- The e-mail address you used
- What you tried and the exact message the app showed
- A screenshot of the screen, if you can
