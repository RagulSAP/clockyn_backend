A **manager** is a staff member you promote so they can help run part of your company. A manager only works within the branches and departments you give them, and only sees the settings you allow. This guide covers the three roles and how to manage managers.

## The three roles

- **Admin** owns the company and can use every screen, including plans, company settings and managers.
- **Manager** is a staff member you promote. A manager sees only the Admin Settings shortcuts you allowed through permissions, for the branches and departments you assigned.
- **Staff** punch attendance, send requests and view their own records. Staff never see admin shortcuts.

## Make someone a manager

This is done by the company admin.

1. Open **Settings > Managers** and tap **Assign Manager** (or **+**).
2. Optionally filter by Branch and Department, then choose the **Staff Member**.
3. Under **Assign To** pick at least one branch and one department.
4. Tap **Assign Manager**.
5. Then set what they may do with the permissions button.

A manager needs at least one branch and one department. The form says "A manager needs at least one branch and one department". Pick every department to cover a whole branch. An Access Preview shows the combinations.

## Choose a manager's permissions

You can choose per manager from 10 permissions:

- Staff Management
- Branch Management
- Attendance Reports
- Salary Management
- Holiday Management
- Shift Management
- Leads Management
- Incentive Management
- Company Settings
- Requests Management

To change them:

1. Open Settings > Managers.
2. Tap the shield icon on the manager.
3. Switch permissions on or off (**Select All** is available).
4. Tap **Save Permissions**.

The sheet shows how many permissions are active, for example 3/10. A manager sees a shortcut in Settings only for the permissions you switched on.

## What managers see

In the Settings tab, a manager sees an **Admin Settings** block with shortcuts only for the permissions you granted. Examples are Add Staff and View Staff, Add Branch and View Branch, Attendance Reports, Holiday Management or Requests Management. Below are General Settings: Profile Settings, App Permissions, Appearance and More Options. Staff see only the General Settings. Managers see the Home, CRM, Chat and Settings tabs.

## See who a manager looks after

On Settings > Managers use **View Branch** on the manager's card to see the assigned branches with their departments. Use the people icon to see the staff under that manager. The top shows Total managers, Assigned and Assignments.

## Remove a manager

1. Open Settings > Managers.
2. Tap the trash icon on the manager.
3. Confirm **Remove**.

The person becomes normal staff again and their branch and department assignments are removed. They stay in your staff list.

> **Note** A manager who still has staff reporting to them cannot be deleted as a staff member. Reassign those staff to another manager first, or use Settings > Managers to remove the manager role.

## Common questions

### Why does a manager need a branch and a department?
Managers only work within the branches and departments you assign. The form asks for at least one of each.

### How do managers sign in?
Managers sign in with **Employee Access**, like staff. If a manager picks Admin Portal, the app tells them to use the other portal.

### Can a manager buy or change a plan?
No. Plans and billing are admin features, and none of the 10 manager permissions covers them. Only the company admin can buy, renew or change a plan.

### What happens to a manager's staff if I remove the manager role?
The person becomes normal staff and their branch and department assignments are removed. Their own details stay in your staff list.

### Why can't I delete a manager?
A manager who still has staff reporting to them cannot be deleted. The app says how many staff still report to them. Reassign those staff first.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A manager cannot see a feature | The permission is switched off | Open Settings > Managers, tap the shield icon and switch it on |
| Assign Manager will not continue | No branch or department selected | Pick at least one of each under Assign To |
| Delete manager is refused | Staff still report to them | Reassign those staff, or remove the manager role instead |

## When to contact support

If a manager role will not save or a permission does not take effect, ask in Support > Live Chat. Have these ready:

- Your company name and admin e-mail
- The manager's name and the branches and departments assigned
- What you changed and what the app showed, with a screenshot if you can
