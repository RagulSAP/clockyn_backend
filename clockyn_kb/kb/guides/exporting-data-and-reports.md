Clockyn can turn your attendance, salary and employee data into reports you can open on screen and download. This guide shows where the reports are, how to generate one, which file formats you can choose, and where your earlier downloads are kept. It covers reports only; there is no screen in the app that exports all of your account's data at once.

## Where to find reports

Open Settings > View & Download Reports, or tap View Reports in the Home quick actions. There are two tabs:

- Generate: the list of report types
- View Reports: the reports you generated earlier

The report types are grouped like this:

| Group | Available reports |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance Reports | Monthly Summary, Staff-wise Detailed Report, Late Report, Overtime Report |
| Salary Reports | Monthly Salary, View Staff Salary |
| Employee Reports | Employee Details, Employee Shift / Timing |

Some rows are greyed out and cannot be opened yet: Incentives, Loss of Pay and Reimbursement (Salary Reports), Previous Employees and Employees Salary Structures (Employee Reports), and Meeting Report, Travel Report and Expense Report (CRM).

## Generate the monthly attendance report

1. Open the reports and choose Monthly Summary.
2. Pick the month and year.
3. Choose All staff or one employee.
4. Tap Generate & View PDF.

The PDF opens and is saved in View Reports for later. If it cannot open, the app says "Could not open the report. It was saved to View Reports."

## Other attendance reports

- **Staff-wise Detailed Report:** one row per employee for the chosen month, with working days, present, absent, late, half day, leave, Sundays and the hours worked, along with branch and department.
- **Late Report:** each late day of the month with employee, branch, department, shift start, punch-in time and how late they were (Late By, for example 25 min or 1h 5m). When shift times are missing, the shift and Late By cells show a dash.
- **Overtime Report:** each employee's overtime and the amount earned, based on the Overtime setting in Salary Structure. Employees without overtime appear in grey. Days with a forgotten punch-out are left out until the admin corrects them.

## Salary and employee reports

Monthly Salary and View Staff Salary show the salary figures for a chosen month. See [how salary is calculated and paid](kb:payroll-and-salary). Employee Details lists your employees with employee ID, department and branch; tap an employee to open the profile report. Employee Shift / Timing shows the shifts.

## Download a report

1. Open the report and tap Export.
2. Choose a format: Download as PDF, Download as DOC, Download as Excel or Download as CSV.

The salary screen (View Staff Salary) offers PDF, DOC and Excel only, without CSV. Your earlier downloads appear in the View Reports tab with their type, such as PDF or Excel, so you can open them again. If nothing is there the app says "No reports yet" and asks you to use the Generate tab.

## What managers can open

Give the manager the Attendance Reports permission under Managers. The manager then finds a reports entry in their Settings and can open Monthly Summary, Late Report, Monthly Salary and View Staff Salary, limited to their staff. Other reports stay admin-only.

## Common questions

### Can I download reports in Excel or CSV?

Yes. The Export sheet offers PDF, DOC, Excel and CSV. The monthly attendance report is generated as a PDF. Salary sheets offer PDF, DOC and Excel.

### Where are my earlier downloads saved?

In the View Reports tab. Every report you generated appears there with its type.

### Why is my report empty or failing to download?

Check that you picked the right month and that staff have attendance in it. If the screen shows an error, tap Retry. A failed download says "Failed to generate PDF" or "Failed to download CSV". Try again after checking your internet connection.

### Why are some report rows greyed out?

They are not available yet. The available reports are in the table above.

### How can a manager see reports?

Give the manager the Attendance Reports permission. The manager then sees a reports entry in Settings.

### Can I get the report for an earlier month?

Yes. Pick the month and year on the report. Unpaid salary months are recalculated from that month's data; paid months show the saved figures.

## What can go wrong

- **The report is empty.** Cause: wrong month, or no attendance in it. Fix: pick the right month and check the staff list.
- **"Failed to generate PDF" or "Failed to download CSV".** Cause: connection problem or a temporary fault. Fix: tap Retry after checking the internet connection.
- **No reports in View Reports.** Cause: none were generated on this account yet. Fix: use the Generate tab.
- **A manager cannot see Reports.** Cause: the Attendance Reports permission is off. Fix: switch it on under Managers.

## When to contact support

If a report keeps failing after you retried, contact Clockyn support through Support > Live Chat. Have ready your company name, the admin e-mail, the report name, the month and year, the exact message on the screen and a screenshot.
