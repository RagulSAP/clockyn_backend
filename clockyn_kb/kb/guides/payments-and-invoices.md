You pay for your Clockyn plan in a payment window that opens from the app. After your payment is confirmed, your plan updates and an invoice is created. This guide covers paying, what to do when something looks wrong, and your invoices.

## How to pay

1. Pick a plan and a staff count (see [Buying, renewing and changing plans](kb:buying-renewing-and-changing-plans)).
2. On the **Payment Summary**, check the lines and the **Grand Total**.
3. Tap **Continue to Pay** and finish the payment in the payment window that opens.
4. When the payment is confirmed you see a success message, and your plan details update.

On iPhone and iPad the app only shows your plan status, so buying is done on an Android phone or the web version.

## Apply a coupon

1. On the Payment Summary find the coupon box.
2. Type your code in **Enter coupon code** and apply it.
3. If it is valid, the discount shows in the price breakdown and the Grand Total updates.

Coupons apply to the staff charge after any plan credit, and the discount is taken before GST. They are not available for the add-staff **Upgrade Plan** flow. A purchase can use a coupon or a referral reward, not both. A coupon can be rejected because the code is wrong or expired, not active yet, not valid for the plan you chose, or because your order is below the coupon's minimum amount. The message under the coupon box says which one applies.

## If a payment does not go as expected

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Payment Cancelled** | You closed the payment window before paying | Nothing was charged. Start again when ready |
| **Verification Failed** or **Payment not confirmed yet** | The payment may have gone through, but the app could not confirm it, for example because of a network drop | Do not pay again. Tap **Retry verification** on the Payment Summary |
| Money debited, plan not updated | The confirmation has not reached your account yet | Open the Payment Summary and tap Retry verification if shown. Wait a short while. If the plan is still not updated, contact support |
| Payment shown as under review | The amount received does not match the amount expected for the order, so our team checks it | You do not need to pay again. Share the details with support |

Our system also receives a confirmation directly from the payment provider, so a successful payment is normally applied automatically within a short time.

### Will I be charged twice if I tap pay again?
Each time you start a payment, a new payment order is created, so a second attempt is a second charge if it succeeds. If you see Payment not confirmed yet or Verification Failed, use **Retry verification** instead of paying again.

## Your invoices

Invoices are created only after a payment is successfully confirmed.

1. Open **Settings > Your Plan & Validity** and tap **Download Invoices**.
2. The screen has two tabs: **Invoice History** lists every invoice from your successful payments, and **Downloaded** lists the invoice files saved on this device.
3. In Invoice History, pick the invoice and choose to download it. The file then appears in the Downloaded tab, where you can open the PDF or share it.

To share an invoice by e-mail or messaging app, go to the Downloaded tab, pick the invoice and choose the share option. To delete a downloaded file, pick it and choose **Remove from this device**, then confirm. This only removes the file from your phone. The invoice stays in Invoice History.

Invoice numbers look like INV-2025-0001: the word INV, the year and a running number. The invoice shows your plan, the amount and the GST split, either as CGST and SGST or as IGST.

## Common questions

### I do not see an invoice for my last payment.
Invoices are created only after a payment is confirmed. If your payment is still unconfirmed or under review, the invoice does not appear yet. Pull down to refresh Invoice History. If the plan is active but the invoice is missing, contact support.

### The app says "No PDF viewer found". What do I do?
Your phone has no app that can open PDF files. Install a PDF reader from your app store, or use the share option to send the invoice to your e-mail or another device.

### Can I get a refund?
Refund rules are decided by the Clockyn team and are not set inside the app. Please contact Clockyn support in Support > Live Chat with your payment details and they will tell you what is possible.

### Is the coupon a percentage or a fixed amount?
It depends on the coupon. Some give a percentage off and some a fixed rupee amount, and a percentage coupon may have an upper limit. The Payment Summary shows exactly how much you saved.

### I closed the payment window. Was I charged?
If you closed it before paying, the app shows Payment Cancelled and nothing is charged. If you paid and closed it afterwards, wait a few minutes and check Settings > Your Plan & Validity.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coupon not accepted | Wrong or expired code, wrong plan, or order below the minimum | Read the message under the coupon box, check the spelling, or try another plan |
| Invoice missing | Payment not yet confirmed | Refresh Invoice History, then contact support if the plan is active |
| Charged twice | A second payment was started and completed | Contact support with the details |

## When to contact support

For money debited without a plan update, a payment under review, a double charge or a refund request, please contact Clockyn support in Support > Live Chat. Have these ready:

- Your company name and admin e-mail
- The date and time of the payment, the amount, and the last digits of the transaction reference
- A screenshot of the Payment Summary or your bank message

Support will check the payment and tell you what is possible. Please do not pay again while a payment is unconfirmed.
